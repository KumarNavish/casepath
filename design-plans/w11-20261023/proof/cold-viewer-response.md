1. **What does it do?**  
CasePath helps a legal protection case handler turn customer documents into an assessment draft. The example concerns a rent increase with missing reasons. It identifies a possible basis for invalidity, shows unresolved questions, and leaves the final decision and customer response to a person.

2. **How does it reach results?**  
Automated assistants read documents, highlight relevant passages, connect facts to legal requirements, and report which checks passed or failed. The sequence distinguishes the original notice from a later email. I can inspect the underlying documents and legal text. A later case reuses three checklist items while checking that case’s own facts and dates.

3. **Why is it valuable?**  
The clearest benefit is a reviewable path from evidence to conclusion. A handler can see why an issue was flagged and what still needs attention. Reusing checks could reduce repetitive work and improve consistency. The video suggests these benefits but does not demonstrate measured time savings or accuracy.

4. **What was unclear?**  
The legal detail sometimes overwhelms the business story. Dense paragraphs, German and English mixed together, and words running together make silent viewing demanding. I understand the missing-reasons finding more readily than some of the assistant roles.

I did not see how a reusable checklist is created, approved, or kept current. I also did not see document intake, a person correcting an assessment, or completion of the unresolved deadline questions. “All files received” alongside missing reasons initially needs interpretation: the file set is complete, but a legal requirement is unmet. The fictional replay is clearly labelled; it does not establish how reliably the product handles a new real case.
