# Cold-viewer response

A fresh reviewer, with no task history, received only the 30 evenly spaced
frames listed in [frames.json](frames.json), taken from the 180 s cut that
introduced case openers, case-named narration, the reply moment and file
icons. Its brief: a busy, non-technical insurance executive seeing CasePath for
the first time in a silent video. Its structured response is kept verbatim
below. The receipt lists which points were fixed before the final cut.

---

## Summary

What CasePath does: a legal-protection customer sends a message and files; CasePath picks a checklist it learned from earlier cases, finds the sentence in each document that answers each question, gives an answer with proof, and drafts a reply that is not sent until a person decides. It reuses checklists on new cases of the same kind. I saw 3 cases. Case 1 (Elin Ziegler): her rent went up on a form that gave no reason. CasePath decides the increase is not valid and drafts a reply. Case 2 (an unnamed tenant): a photo of mould that keeps coming back, with CasePath marking the spots. I never saw what caused it or what to do. Case 3 (Drew Shaw): the same rent problem as case 1, solved with the same checklist, but I never saw its result. Case 1 takes about two-thirds of the film, frames 1-20. Cases 2 and 3 are rushed, and the final 'All 9 cases / 15 uses' count does not match what I watched. I will remember 'Every answer shows its proof. A person always decides.', the pink highlighted proof sentences, the dark narration cards, and 'learned once, reused'. The email draft was not memorable because it reads like legal notes, not a letter.

## Findings

- **frames 21-22 (and the knowledge map in frames 28-29)** (blocker)
  - Problem: Case 2 (mould) is never finished. It appears for about 2 frames and the 'What CasePath found' panel stays empty (frame 22). There is no answer, no cause, no draft, and no 'not sent' step before the cut to case 3. It is also missing from the knowledge map: the 'Defects and repairs' row lists only heating cases. The opening question 'What is causing it?' is never answered.
  - Suggested fix: Before case 3, hold on case 2 long enough to fill 'What CasePath found' with one plain finding and end on one answer or next action. Then show which checklist that case used or fed on the knowledge map.
- **frame 23 vs frame 2; frames 24-25 then 26** (major)
  - Problem: Case 3 copies case 1 word for word: the same problem sentence ('The rent went up on a form that gives no reason.') and the same 4 file names. It looks like a replay of case 1. Its result is never shown before the film cuts to the knowledge page, so the benefit of reuse (faster? same answer?) is not visible.
  - Suggested fix: Give case 3 a slightly different problem line and file name, plus a 3-5 second ending: all 3 questions checked, the verdict, and one line saying what was reused (for example, 'No new checklist needed').
- **frames 26, 27, 28, 29** (major)
  - Problem: The narration says 'ALL 9 CASES' and the counters reach '9 cases / 15 uses', but the viewer saw only 3 cases. Six new cases and a 'Lease termination' checklist appear from nowhere. 'Uses' is not explained: 15 uses from 9 cases is confusing. Frame 27 also shows '0 uses' right after the film said a checklist was reused.
  - Suggested fix: Say this is a library and include the films' cases, for example 'Our library: 9 example cases, 3 shown here'. Mark cases 1-3 on the map, define 'use' or drop that counter, and start the counters at their final values.
- **frames 19-20** (major)
  - Problem: The reply draft does not read like an email to a customer. It has no greeting and no sign-off. The lines are fragments, one ending in ';' and one starting in lower case ('the later market reply remains contextual evidence.'). It quotes a legal article ('CO 269d(2)(b)'). The subject is German, cut off ('…Begründu...') and the body English. An executive cannot picture sending it, so the 'facts become action' moment falls flat.
  - Suggested fix: Show the draft as a short letter: a greeting, one plain sentence per finding (for example, 'Your landlord gave no reason with the increase, so it is not valid.'), one next step, and a sign-off. Keep the lines that link each finding to its sentence, and use one language.
- **frames 3, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14** (major)
  - Problem: The document text looks like raw machine output. It shows ISO timestamps ('2025-11-24T12:00:00+01:00', '2025-11-18T16:20:00+01:00'), a file name with page notation ('Mietzinserhoehung_Formular_Elin-Ziegler_2025-11-18.pdf: all three pages 1/3,2/3,3/3') and missing spaces ('Article269c', 'FromVerde Gestion SA', 'Date2025-11-21'). It also shows broken PDF extraction ('pro Monat / Fr. / Fr. / pro Monat', 'Begleitschreiben1'). The customer's email is in German, but the 'Lease and original notice' file is in English and contains her request ('My active request is assessment of the original notice's missing-reasons/nullity ground…', frame 12). The documents look made up.
  - Suggested fix: Format dates as 24.11.2025, add the missing spaces, hide file names and page notation, keep each file in one language, and only scroll to clean passages near the highlighted sentence.
- **frames 8-13** (major)
  - Problem: The progress shown contradicts itself and the order jumps. Frame 9 says 'Evidence checklist 1 of 3 checked' next to two green ticks, and the right panel says '2 done' while the listed items are unticked. The questions go Q2 → Q3 (frame 10, where Q2's circle is empty again) → 'Question 1 answered' (frame 11) → Q2 again (frames 12-13) with its checklist reset to unticked. It looks like the work is being redone.
  - Suggested fix: Show the questions in order 1 → 2 → 3, keep finished circles marked 'Checked', and drive the 'x of 3 checked' and 'n done' counts from the same state as the ticks.
- **frame 18 (also 10, 12)** (major)
  - Problem: The verdict screen uses lawyer language an executive will not follow: 'omitted substantive reasons, supporting invalidity under CO 269d(2)(b); the later market reply remains contextual evidence'. Other screens are similar: 'retained as adverse/contextual evidence', 'mechanically reproduced signature is not relied on as a defect', 'missing-reasons/nullity ground and preparation of a protective objection'.
  - Suggested fix: Keep the plain headline ('The rent increase is not valid: no reasons were given with it.') as the main text. Make the legal citation small, secondary text, or leave it out of the film.
- **frames 4, 5, 24, 28** (major)
  - Problem: 'What CasePath learned' is unclear. Case 1 already uses a checklist 'learned from an earlier case' ('A blank reason field and a later market email'), a case the viewer never sees, so no learning happens on screen. The knowledge map also renames the cases: case 1 becomes 'An increase form with missing original reasons' and case 3 'An increase form and the later email chronology', not Elin Ziegler or Drew Shaw. The viewer cannot link the map to the cases just watched.
  - Suggested fix: On the map, label the cases 'Case 1 · Elin Ziegler' and 'Case 3 · Drew Shaw' with the same problem lines used in the film. Add one narration line saying where the checklist came from, for example 'Learned from an earlier rent case.'
- **frames 2, 3, 9, 10, 12, 13, 16, 17, 22, 23, 25** (major)
  - Problem: About a third of the sampled frames have no narration card, including the densest screens: the German email in frame 3, the PDF extraction in frame 9, the long lease text in frames 12-13, and the photo with marked spots in frame 22. Frames 16-17 show an 'Answer' heading over blank space and faded rows for about 12 s before the verdict appears. Here the viewer does not know where to look or why it matters.
  - Suggested fix: Keep a short narration card on screen in every scene. Show the verdict line as soon as the Answer screen opens, or cut the empty build-up to about 2 s.
- **frames 6, 25, 11, 4** (minor)
  - Problem: The evidence logic looks wrong. 'The lease — For question 2: Did the landlord give reasons?' suggests the lease proves whether reasons were given with the increase. The checklist card says '3 documents', but 'Evidence this case needs' lists only one (frames 6 and 25). The lease is tagged 'Proof for question 2' but is highlighted to answer question 1 (frame 11).
  - Suggested fix: List all 3 needed documents, each against the question it answers (form → Q2, lease → Q1, later email → Q3), and use the same tags in the sidebar.
- **frames 7, 8, 12, 13, 15, 26-29** (minor)
  - Problem: The top tabs (Claim reader, Source checker, Process builder, Document finder, Result checker) switch with no visible reason. The same Q2 check runs under 'Document finder' (frame 8), 'Source checker' (frame 12) and 'Process builder' (frame 13). The knowledge page sits under 'Process builder'. Only one tooltip (frame 7) explains a tab, so an executive cannot tell what the tabs mean.
  - Suggested fix: Mention the five assistants once in the opening, or name the working assistant inside the narration card (for example, 'Document finder: checks each file').
- **frame 24** (minor)
  - Problem: The highlighted 'Rent increases' card covers the knowledge heading ('…ecklists CasePath has learned' is cut off) and sits on top of another card. That card's '3 documents / 4 checks / Learned from "An emailed termination…"' shows underneath, so the frame looks glitchy.
  - Suggested fix: Lift the card into empty space, or fade the library behind it, so the heading and the other cards are not partly covered.
- **frames 8, 11, 18** (minor)
  - Problem: The narration connector lines run diagonally through the text being read. They cross the highlighted lease sentence in frames 8 and 11 and run through 'CO 269d' and 'undetermined' in frame 18.
  - Suggested fix: Route the connector around text blocks (along the margin), or end it at the edge of the highlighted element.
- **frames 21-23 vs 1-2** (minor)
  - Problem: Case identity is inconsistent. Case 2's customer has no name in the sidebar and is called 'A tenant' in the case strip, while cases 1 and 3 have named customers. The 'Customer's message' icon is a chat bubble in case 1 and an envelope in cases 2 and 3.
  - Suggested fix: Give case 2 a named customer with the name in the sidebar, and use one icon for 'Customer's message' in all cases.
