## Pacing and hierarchy decisions

- Significant new contexts receive an anticipatory bridge or a deliberate orientation hold before meaningful interaction.
- Explanations state why the component matters, rather than only naming it.
- Spotlight labels sit above the source window, preserving source text.
- Recorded excerpts and authored explanations have visibly different roles.
- Source views are reframed from a clean 4K capture for a 1080p export.
- Major titles fit a single line; captions fit a single line; the source stage begins below the editorial copy.
- The opening and closing four-part journey avoid redundant descriptor text.

- Music supports the chapter timing. All necessary meaning remains visual.
