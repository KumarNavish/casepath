1. **What does the product do?**  
   CasePath helps a legal protection case handler turn customer documents into an assessment they can review. The example concerns a rent increase: it identifies missing original reasons, connects that finding to a legal provision, and prepares an assessment draft. The handler remains responsible for the decision and reply. The closing screen suggests coverage of tenancy repairs, lease termination and rent increases.

2. **How does it reach its results?**  
   Automated assistants read documents, highlight relevant passages, check quotations against sources, and connect facts and law to specific questions. The result shows which legal tests passed or failed and what remains unresolved. It also reuses a checklist from an earlier similar case while checking the new case’s documents and facts afresh. That reuse mechanism is understandable in frames 17–20.

3. **Why is it valuable?**  
   I can see the potential to reduce document searching and repeated preparation, make reviews more consistent, and let a supervisor trace an assessment back to its evidence. Keeping uncertainty visible and leaving the draft unsent are valuable controls. Reusing a checklist could help retain expertise across cases. The images establish the proposed workflow; they do not establish time savings, accuracy improvements or financial returns.
