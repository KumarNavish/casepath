## Decision standard

**Complete** means the observable intake sources suffice to assess the material
facts, applicable requirements, relevant documents and time limits of the
customer's actual requested handling path. It is not a court success prediction,
and it does not require every document in a catalog or resolution of every
immaterial question. A sufficient reliable account can establish a fact without
a document that the law does not require. Conflicting or expressly uncertain
accounts remain unknown unless another actual source resolves the conflict.

**Legally founded** means at least one identified tenant request/objection has
source-supported factual predicates under the applicable legal provision.
`founded_limited_received_notice_objection` records a supported issue concerning
the particular received landlord notice; it does not assert that all requested
relief, procedural timeliness, all communications or the tenancy outcome are
established. Limited reported repair/threat grounds likewise accept cognizable
firsthand statements as evidence, while preserving source confidence and
broader unresolved predicates. `indeterminate` means missing proof or applicability prevents that
broader conclusion. No row equates missing proof with a failed legal condition.
Five repaired-leak files ask for administrative record/closure: that is a proper
limited handling task, not a new live substantive remedy. Their repair-and-dry
reports are adverse to a new removal demand, not to administrative closure.

**A complete and founded nomination** requires both completeness and foundation
for its disclosed active legal path. A defective landlord notice can provide the
tenant's challenge ground. Its invalidity never makes the tenant claim invalid.
No originals qualify as complete substantive legal-merit demonstrations;
there are0qualified originals per domain, a gap of3per domain. Five repaired

repair-and-dry account. Their exact historic duration is unknown but does not
