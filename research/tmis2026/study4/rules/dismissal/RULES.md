# Rule set: dismissal (version 2026-11-12.2)

Development material for Study 4, step 2a. Every clause cites the Swiss Code of Obligations (SR 220) in the consolidated version applicable from 2026-10-01 (Fedlex). The English translation is quoted; the German text, which is authoritative, is quoted beside it. Passages are verbatim from `sources/co-sr220-en.txt` and `sources/co-sr220-de.txt`. A document is needed when its step is reached and its condition holds.

## Notice of termination by the employer during a protected period (CO art. 336c)

Type id `protected_period_notice`; defining event `employer_gave_notice`.

### Deciding conditions

| fact | question | not this | citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `employer_gave_notice` | Did the employer give the employee notice of termination of the employment relationship? | A resignation by the employee, or a fixed-term relationship that ended by running out. | CO art. 336c para. 1 (introduction) [states]: “After the probation period has expired, the employer may not terminate the employment relationship” (DE: „Nach Ablauf der Probezeit darf der Arbeitgeber das Arbeitsverhältnis nicht kündigen“) |
| `notice_after_probation` | Was the notice given after the probation period had expired? | Notice given while the probation period was still running, even if the employee was ill, pregnant or in service at the time. | CO art. 336c para. 1 (introduction) [states]: “After the probation period has expired, the employer may not terminate the employment relationship” (DE: „Nach Ablauf der Probezeit darf der Arbeitgeber das Arbeitsverhältnis nicht kündigen“) |
| `probation_varied` | Does the file say that a written individual agreement, a standard employment contract or a collective employment contract set a probation period other than the first month of employment? | An oral arrangement about probation, or a remark only that the employee was still new. | CO art. 335b para. 2 [states]: “Different terms may be envisaged by an individual written agreement, a standard employment contract or a collective employment contract; however, the probation period may not exceed three months.” (DE: „Durch schriftliche Abrede, Normalarbeitsvertrag oder Gesamtarbeitsvertrag können abweichende Vereinbarungen getroffen werden; die Probezeit darf jedoch auf höchstens drei Monate verlängert werden.“) |
| `incapacity_illness_accident` | Was the employee, through no fault of their own, wholly or partly prevented from working by illness or accident on the day notice was given or while the notice period was still running? | Incapacity the employee caused through their own fault, incapacity that ended before notice was given, or illness that began only after the notice period had ended. | CO art. 336c para. 1 let. b [states]: “while the employee through no fault of his own is partially or entirely prevented from working by illness or accident” (DE: „während der Arbeitnehmer ohne eigenes Verschulden durch Krankheit oder durch Unfall ganz oder teilweise an der Arbeitsleistung verhindert ist“) |
| `compulsory_service` | Was the employee performing Swiss compulsory military service, civil defence service or alternative civilian service on the day notice was given or while the notice period was running, or, for such a service lasting more than eleven days, did either of those fall within the four weeks before or after it? | Voluntary service, service in a foreign army, or a service of eleven days or less that did not overlap the notice day or the notice period. | CO art. 336c para. 1 let. a [states]: “while the other party is performing Swiss compulsory military or civil defence service or Swiss alternative civilian service or, where such service lasts for more than eleven days, during the four weeks preceding or following it” (DE: „während die andere Partei schweizerischen obligatorischen Militär- oder Schutzdienst oder schweizerischen Zivildienst leistet, sowie, sofern die Dienstleistung mehr als elf Tage dauert, während vier Wochen vorher und nachher“) |
| `pregnancy_or_birth` | Was the employee pregnant, or within sixteen weeks after giving birth, on the day notice was given or while the notice period was running? | Pregnancy of the employee's partner; the further leave periods of art. 336c para. 1 let. cbis to cquinquies, which this rule set does not cover. | CO art. 336c para. 1 let. c [states]: “during the pregnancy of an employee and the sixteen weeks following birth” (DE: „während der Schwangerschaft und in den 16 Wochen nach der Niederkunft einer Arbeitnehmerin“) |
| `notice_before_protected_period` | Was the notice given before the protected period began, with the notice period not yet expired when it began? | Notice given on a day that already fell inside the protected period (that notice is void, not suspended). | CO art. 336c para. 2 [states]: “where such notice was given prior to the commencement of a proscribed period but the notice period has not yet expired at that juncture, it is suspended and does not resume until the proscribed period has ended” (DE: „ist dagegen die Kündigung vor Beginn einer solchen Frist erfolgt, aber die Kündigungsfrist bis dahin noch nicht abgelaufen, so wird deren Ablauf unterbrochen und erst nach Beendigung der Sperrfrist fortgesetzt“) |
| `notice_period_varied` | Does the file say that a written individual employment contract, a standard employment contract or a collective employment contract sets notice periods different from the statutory ones (one month in the first year of service, two months in the second to ninth years, three months thereafter)? | A notice period that only the termination letter states, with no written contract term behind it. | CO art. 335c para. 2 [states]: “These notice periods may be varied by written individual, standard or collective employment contract” (DE: „Diese Fristen dürfen durch schriftliche Abrede, Normalarbeitsvertrag oder Gesamtarbeitsvertrag abgeändert werden“) |

| `a_assess` Assess the effect of the notice (void, suspended or valid) | employer_gave_notice = yes and notice_after_probation = yes | CO art. 336c para. 2 [follows]: “Any notice of termination given during the proscribed periods stipulated in paragraph 1 is void” (DE: „Die Kündigung, die während einer der in Absatz 1 festgesetzten Sperrfristen erklärt wird, ist nichtig“) Inference: Paragraph 2 gives one of two effects (void or suspended) to notice and a protected period, so both branches end in one assessment of that effect. |

| `medical_certificate` Medical certificate of incapacity for work | `a_incapacity` | always | cause (illness or accident); full or partial incapacity; first and last day of incapacity; date issued | CO art. 336c para. 1 let. b [evidence]: “while the employee through no fault of his own is partially or entirely prevented from working by illness or accident” (DE: „während der Arbeitnehmer ohne eigenes Verschulden durch Krankheit oder durch Unfall ganz oder teilweise an der Arbeitsleistung verhindert ist“) Inference: The bar applies only while the employee is prevented from working by illness or accident; a medical certificate is the ordinary proof of that incapacity and its dates, to compare with the notice. |
| `service_confirmation` Official confirmation of compulsory service | `a_service` | always | kind of service (military, civil defence, alternative civilian); first and last day of service | CO art. 336c para. 1 let. a [evidence]: “while the other party is performing Swiss compulsory military or civil defence service or Swiss alternative civilian service or, where such service lasts for more than eleven days, during the four weeks preceding or following it” (DE: „während die andere Partei schweizerischen obligatorischen Militär- oder Schutzdienst oder schweizerischen Zivildienst leistet, sowie, sofern die Dienstleistung mehr als elf Tage dauert, während vier Wochen vorher und nachher“) Inference: The bar depends on the kind of service, its dates and whether it lasted more than eleven days; the official service order or confirmation ordinarily shows all three. |
| `pregnancy_certificate` Medical confirmation of pregnancy or record of the birth | `a_pregnancy` | always | pregnancy confirmed and its dates, or the date of birth | CO art. 336c para. 1 let. c [evidence]: “during the pregnancy of an employee and the sixteen weeks following birth” (DE: „während der Schwangerschaft und in den 16 Wochen nach der Niederkunft einer Arbeitnehmerin“) Inference: The bar runs during pregnancy and sixteen weeks after the birth; a medical confirmation of the pregnancy or the birth record ordinarily dates that period. |
| `notice_period_terms` Written notice-period clause (individual, standard or collective contract) | `a_suspension` | notice_period_varied = yes | notice period length; end-point of the notice (e.g. end of a month); the written instrument that sets it (individual, standard or collective contract) | CO art. 336c para. 3 [evidence]: “Where a specific end-point, such as the end of a month or working week, has been set for termination of the employment relationship and such end-point does not coincide with the expiry of the resumed notice period, the latter is extended until the next applicable end-point.” Inference: Resuming and extending a suspended notice period needs its length and end-point; where a written contract varies the statutory periods, that clause is the ordinary proof of them. |

### Presumptions

| fact | value | applies | citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `probation_varied` | no | unstated | CO art. 335b para. 1 [follows]: “the probation period is considered to be the first month of an employment relationship” (DE: „als Probezeit gilt der erste Monat eines Arbeitsverhältnisses“) Inference: The statute deems the probation period to be the first month and paragraph 2 allows other terms only in writing, so where the file states no such written term the first month applies. |
| `notice_period_varied` | no | unstated | CO art. 335c para. 1 [follows]: “The employment relationship may be terminated at one month’s notice during the first year of service, at two months’ notice in the second to ninth years of service and at three months’ notice thereafter, all such notice to expire at the end of a calendar month.” Inference: Paragraph 1 fixes the notice periods by law and paragraph 2 lets them differ only by a written individual, standard or collective contract, so where the file states no such written term the statutory periods apply. |

Every other fact except the defining event `employer_gave_notice` falls under the convention of this benchmark (not law), which is not a passage of law: a branch fact that the file does not mention is taken as not alleged, and the rules are applied as if it were absent; the defining event is never presumed: if the file does not mention it, it is unresolved; a fact the file raises and leaves unsettled is open.

## Wrongful (abusive) notice of termination by the employer: objection and compensation claim (CO arts. 336-336b)

Type id `wrongful_termination`; defining event `employer_gave_notice`.

### Deciding conditions

| fact | question | not this | citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `employer_gave_notice` | Did the employer give the employee notice of termination of the employment relationship? | A resignation by the employee, or a fixed-term relationship that ended by running out. | CO art. 336b para. 2 [states]: “the party on whom notice was served may bring his claim for compensation” (DE: „so kann die Partei, der gekündigt worden ist, ihren Anspruch auf Entschädigung geltend machen“) |
| `notice_period_ended` | Has the notice period (from the notice to the end of the employment relationship) already ended? | The employee's last working day where they were released from work while the notice period was still running. | CO art. 336b para. 1 [states]: “A party seeking compensation pursuant to Articles 336 and 336a must submit his objection to the notice of termination in writing to the party giving such notice not later than the end of the notice period.” (DE: „Wer gestützt auf Artikel 336 und 336a eine Entschädigung geltend machen will, muss gegen die Kündigung längstens bis zum Ende der Kündigungsfrist beim Kündigenden schriftlich Einsprache erheben.“) |
| `objection_submitted` | Did the employee send the employer a written objection to the notice before the notice period ended? | An oral protest, or a written objection sent only after the notice period had ended. | CO art. 336b para. 1 [states]: “A party seeking compensation pursuant to Articles 336 and 336a must submit his objection to the notice of termination in writing to the party giving such notice not later than the end of the notice period.” (DE: „Wer gestützt auf Artikel 336 und 336a eine Entschädigung geltend machen will, muss gegen die Kündigung längstens bis zum Ende der Kündigungsfrist beim Kündigenden schriftlich Einsprache erheben.“) |
| `more_than_180_days` | Have more than 180 days passed since the employment relationship ended? | 180 days counted from the notice or from the last day actually worked rather than from the end of the employment relationship. | CO art. 336b para. 2 [states]: “The claim prescribes if not brought before the courts within 180 days of the end of the employment relationship.” (DE: „Wird nicht innert 180 Tagen nach Beendigung des Arbeitsverhältnisses eine Klage anhängig gemacht, ist der Anspruch verwirkt.“) |
| `action_in_time` | Did the employee bring the compensation claim before a court within 180 days of the end of the employment relationship? | A demand letter to the employer, or a court claim filed only after the 180 days. | CO art. 336b para. 2 [states]: “The claim prescribes if not brought before the courts within 180 days of the end of the employment relationship.” (DE: „Wird nicht innert 180 Tagen nach Beendigung des Arbeitsverhältnisses eine Klage anhängig gemacht, ist der Anspruch verwirkt.“) |
| `reasons_requested` | Did the employee ask the employer to state the reasons for the notice in writing? | Reasons the employer gave without being asked, or a request made only orally to a colleague rather than to the employer. | CO art. 335 para. 2 [states]: “The party giving notice of termination must state his reasons in writing if the other party so requests.” (DE: „Der Kündigende muss die Kündigung schriftlich begründen, wenn die andere Partei dies verlangt.“) |
| `retaliation_for_claims` | Does the employee say the notice was given because they had asserted, in good faith, claims arising from the employment relationship? | Complaints that are not claims under the employment relationship, or a mere coincidence in time that the employee does not link to the notice. | CO art. 336 para. 1 let. d [states]: “because the other party asserts claims under the employment relationship in good faith” (DE: „weil die andere Partei nach Treu und Glauben Ansprüche aus dem Arbeitsverhältnis geltend macht“) |
| `employee_representative` | Was the employee, when notice was given, an elected employee representative on the staff council of the business or on a body linked to the business? | Trade-union membership or union activity without an elected mandate (art. 336 para. 2 let. a, not covered here). | CO art. 336 para. 2 let. b [states]: “while the employee is an elected employee representative on the staff council for the business or on a body linked to the business and the employer cannot cite just cause to terminate his employment” (DE: „während der Arbeitnehmer gewählter Arbeitnehmervertreter in einer betrieblichen oder in einer dem Unternehmen angeschlossenen Einrichtung ist, und der Arbeitgeber nicht beweisen kann, dass er einen begründeten Anlass zur Kündigung hatte“) |

### Steps

| step | reached when | citation |
|---|---|---|
| `b_intake` Establish whether the employer gave notice | always | CO art. 336b para. 2 [follows]: “the party on whom notice was served may bring his claim for compensation” (DE: „so kann die Partei, der gekündigt worden ist, ihren Anspruch auf Entschädigung geltend machen“) Inference: The compensation claim belongs to the party on whom notice was served, so the process starts by establishing that notice. |
| `b_objection_check` Check whether the objection window has closed without a written objection | employer_gave_notice = yes | CO art. 336b para. 1 [states]: “A party seeking compensation pursuant to Articles 336 and 336a must submit his objection to the notice of termination in writing to the party giving such notice not later than the end of the notice period.” (DE: „Wer gestützt auf Artikel 336 und 336a eine Entschädigung geltend machen will, muss gegen die Kündigung längstens bis zum Ende der Kündigungsfrist beim Kündigenden schriftlich Einsprache erheben.“) |
| `b_limit_check` Check the 180-day time limit for the court claim | employer_gave_notice = yes and not (notice_period_ended = yes and objection_submitted = no) | CO art. 336b para. 2 [states]: “The claim prescribes if not brought before the courts within 180 days of the end of the employment relationship.” (DE: „Wird nicht innert 180 Tagen nach Beendigung des Arbeitsverhältnisses eine Klage anhängig gemacht, ist der Anspruch verwirkt.“) |
| `b_notice` Record the notice, the notice period, the objection and any court claim | employer_gave_notice = yes and not (notice_period_ended = yes and objection_submitted = no) and not (more_than_180_days = yes and action_in_time = no) | CO art. 336b para. 1 [follows]: “A party seeking compensation pursuant to Articles 336 and 336a must submit his objection to the notice of termination in writing to the party giving such notice not later than the end of the notice period.” (DE: „Wer gestützt auf Artikel 336 und 336a eine Entschädigung geltend machen will, muss gegen die Kündigung längstens bis zum Ende der Kündigungsfrist beim Kündigenden schriftlich Einsprache erheben.“) Inference: The objection must reach the employer in writing before the notice period ends, so the notice, the period and the objection are recorded together. |
| `b_grounds` Establish the reason the employer gave for the notice | employer_gave_notice = yes and not (notice_period_ended = yes and objection_submitted = no) and not (more_than_180_days = yes and action_in_time = no) | CO art. 336 para. 1 (introduction) [follows]: “Notice of termination is unlawful where given by one party:” (DE: „Die Kündigung eines Arbeitsverhältnisses ist missbräuchlich, wenn eine Partei sie ausspricht:“) Inference: Article 336 makes notice wrongful by the reason for which it was given, so the employer's reason is established before the grounds are examined. |
| `b_retaliation` Examine the claimed retaliation for asserting claims | (employer_gave_notice = yes and not (notice_period_ended = yes and objection_submitted = no) and not (more_than_180_days = yes and action_in_time = no)) and retaliation_for_claims = yes | CO art. 336 para. 1 let. d [states]: “because the other party asserts claims under the employment relationship in good faith” (DE: „weil die andere Partei nach Treu und Glauben Ansprüche aus dem Arbeitsverhältnis geltend macht“) |
| `b_representative` Examine the protection of an elected employee representative | (employer_gave_notice = yes and not (notice_period_ended = yes and objection_submitted = no) and not (more_than_180_days = yes and action_in_time = no)) and employee_representative = yes | CO art. 336 para. 2 let. b [states]: “while the employee is an elected employee representative on the staff council for the business or on a body linked to the business and the employer cannot cite just cause to terminate his employment” (DE: „während der Arbeitnehmer gewählter Arbeitnehmervertreter in einer betrieblichen oder in einer dem Unternehmen angeschlossenen Einrichtung ist, und der Arbeitgeber nicht beweisen kann, dass er einen begründeten Anlass zur Kündigung hatte“) |
| `b_compensation` Assess the compensation claim | employer_gave_notice = yes and not (notice_period_ended = yes and objection_submitted = no) and not (more_than_180_days = yes and action_in_time = no) | CO art. 336a para. 2 [states]: “though it must not exceed an amount equivalent to six months’ salary for the employee” (DE: „darf aber den Betrag nicht übersteigen, der dem Lohn des Arbeitnehmers für sechs Monate entspricht“) |

### Presumptions

None from statute.

Every other fact except the defining event `employer_gave_notice` falls under the convention of this benchmark (not law), which is not a passage of law: a branch fact that the file does not mention is taken as not alleged, and the rules are applied as if it were absent; the defining event is never presumed: if the file does not mention it, it is unresolved; a fact the file raises and leaves unsettled is open.

## Dismissal with immediate effect by the employer (CO arts. 337, 337c)

Type id `immediate_dismissal`; defining event `dismissed_immediately`.

### Deciding conditions

| fact | question | not this | citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dismissed_immediately` | Did the employer end the employment relationship with immediate effect, without a notice period? | Ordinary notice with a notice period, however short, or release from work while a notice period runs. | CO art. 337c para. 1 [states]: “Where the employer dismisses the employee with immediate effect without good cause, the employee is entitled to damages” (DE: „Entlässt der Arbeitgeber den Arbeitnehmer fristlos ohne wichtigen Grund, so hat dieser Anspruch auf Ersatz“) |
| `reasons_requested` | Did the employee ask the employer to state the reasons for the immediate dismissal in writing? | Reasons the employer gave without being asked. | CO art. 337 para. 1 [states]: “the party doing so must give his reasons in writing at the other party’s request” (DE: „er muss die fristlose Vertragsauflösung schriftlich begründen, wenn die andere Partei dies verlangt“) |

| `other_earnings` | Has the employee earned income from other work during the time the notice period or the agreed term would still have run? | Unemployment benefits or other payments that are not earnings from work. | CO art. 337c para. 2 [states]: “Such damages are reduced by any amounts that the employee saved as a result of the termination of the employment relationship or that he earned by performing other work” (DE: „Der Arbeitnehmer muss sich daran anrechnen lassen, was er infolge der Beendigung des Arbeitsverhältnisses erspart hat und was er durch anderweitige Arbeit verdient“) |
