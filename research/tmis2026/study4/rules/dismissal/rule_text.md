# Rules: end of an employment relationship in Switzerland (dismissal)

These rules say which documents a legal-protection insurer requests before it assesses an employee's dispute about the end of an employment relationship. They come from the Swiss Code of Obligations (CO, SR 220), consolidated version of 1 October 2026, and one article of the Civil Procedure Code (CPC, SR 272). The German text is authoritative; German terms are given where the English translation is unclear, and the rules follow the German where the two differ (see below).

There are three dispute types. Each type has a defining event, conditions to decide, steps, documents, presumptions and stops. A step is taken when its condition holds. A document is needed when its step is taken and its own condition holds; every needed document is mandatory. When a stop applies, the process ends and no further step of that type is taken. A presumption fills a fact the file does not mention.

## How to read steps and stops

Steps are taken in the order given. A document belongs to a step and is decided when that step is reached. A stop ends the process at its step: no later step is reached, and no document of a later step is requested or left waiting. Documents of steps reached before the stop keep the state they had; a document requested there remains requested.

## Convention of this benchmark (not law)

The following rule is a convention of this benchmark, not of the law; no article states it:

> A fact that the file does not mention is taken as not alleged, and the rules are applied as if it were absent. Silence never ends a process: where a fact's absence would end it, the unmentioned fact is unresolved. The defining event is never presumed: if the file does not mention it, it is unresolved. A fact that the file raises and leaves unsettled is open.

## Where the translation was not followed

The German text is authoritative. Where the English translation differs from it in legal meaning, the rules follow the German:

- CO art. 336b para. 2: the English says “The claim prescribes if not brought before the courts”; the German says „eine Klage anhängig gemacht ... ist der Anspruch verwirkt“. The rules read it as forfeited ("verwirkt"), which applies by itself, not "prescribes" (a time bar that must be pleaded); the claim must be made pending ("anhängig gemacht"), which under CPC art. 62 para. 1 happens when a conciliation request or an action is filed, read with CPC art. 62 para. 1 (an inference across the two acts: the CO says "anhängig gemacht", the CPC defines "Rechtshängigkeit"), not only "brought before the courts". (Used in: action_in_time, b_stop_time_limit, court_filing.)
- CO art. 336 para. 2 let. b: the English says “the employer cannot cite just cause”; the German says „der Arbeitgeber nicht beweisen kann, dass er einen begründeten Anlass zur Kündigung hatte“. The rules read it as the employer must prove a justified reason ("begründeter Anlass"), a lower standard than the good cause ("wichtiger Grund") of art. 337. (Used in: b_representative.)
- CO art. 336 para. 1: the English says “Notice of termination is unlawful”; the German says „Die Kündigung ... ist missbräuchlich“. The rules read it as abusive ("missbräuchlich"): the notice stays effective and gives rise to compensation (arts. 336a, 336b), it is not void. (Used in: b_grounds, type 2 summary.)
- CO art. 336b para. 1: the English says “submit his objection ... in writing to the party giving such notice”; the German says „beim Kündigenden schriftlich Einsprache erheben“. The rules read it as the objection is lodged with the employer, not merely sent. (Used in: objection_submitted, written_objection.)

## Type 1: Notice of termination by the employer: probation, protected periods and notice period (CO arts. 335b, 335c, 336c)

After the probation period has expired, the employer may not give notice during the protected periods of art. 336c para. 1 ("Sperrfristen"); for illness or accident the protection lasts up to 30 days in the first year of service, 90 days in the second to fifth years and 180 days from the sixth year (let. b). A probation period interrupted by illness, accident or a non-voluntary legal obligation is extended accordingly (art. 335b para. 3). Notice given during a protected period is void ("nichtig", art. 336c para. 2). Notice given before such a period began, with the notice period still running when it began, is suspended and resumes only after the period ends (art. 336c para. 2); if a specific end-point applies, such as the end of a month, the resumed notice period is extended to the next end-point (art. 336c para. 3). Only the protected periods of letters a, b and c are covered.

Defining event: `employer_gave_notice`. It is never presumed.

### Conditions

1. `employer_gave_notice`: Did the employer give the employee notice of termination of the employment relationship? Not: A resignation by the employee, a fixed-term relationship that ended by running out, or a dismissal with immediate effect (art. 337, a separate type). (art. 336c para. 1, introduction)
2. `notice_during_probation`: Was the notice given while the probation period was still running, counting any extension of the probation period under art. 335b para. 3 (a probation period interrupted by illness, accident or a non-voluntary legal obligation is extended accordingly)? Not: Notice given after the probation period, with any such extension, had expired. (art. 336c para. 1, introduction)
3. `probation_varied`: Does the file say that a written individual agreement, a standard employment contract or a collective employment contract set a probation period other than the first month of employment? Not: An oral arrangement about probation, or a remark only that the employee was still new. (art. 335b para. 2)
4. `incapacity_illness_accident`: Was the employee, through no fault of their own, wholly or partly prevented from working by illness or accident on the day notice was given or at some time while the notice period was running, and was the incapacity at that point still within its protected length: up to 30 days in the first year of service, 90 days in the second to fifth years, 180 days from the sixth year? Not: Incapacity the employee caused through their own fault; days of incapacity beyond the 30, 90 or 180 days; incapacity that ended before notice was given or began only after the notice period had ended. (art. 336c para. 1 let. b; para. 2)
5. `compulsory_service`: Was the employee performing Swiss compulsory military service, civil defence service or alternative civilian service on the day notice was given or while the notice period was running, or, for such a service lasting more than eleven days, did either of those fall within the four weeks before or after it? Not: Voluntary service, service in a foreign army, a service of eleven days or less that did not overlap the notice day or the notice period, or an overseas aid project (art. 336c para. 1 let. d, not covered here). (art. 336c para. 1 let. a; para. 2)
6. `pregnancy_or_birth`: Was the employee pregnant, or within sixteen weeks after giving birth, on the day notice was given or while the notice period was running? Not: Pregnancy of the employee's partner; the further leave periods of art. 336c para. 1 let. cbis to cquinquies, which this rule set does not cover. (art. 336c para. 1 let. c; para. 2)
7. `notice_before_protected_period`: Where a protected period (illness or accident, compulsory service, pregnancy or birth, as above) is raised: was the notice given before that period began, with the notice period not yet expired when it began? Not: Notice given on a day that already fell inside the protected period (that notice is void, not suspended). Not asked where no protected period is raised. (art. 336c para. 2) Always stated in the file (stated or open, never left unmentioned).
8. `notice_period_varied`: Does the file say that a written individual employment contract, a standard employment contract or a collective employment contract sets notice periods different from the statutory ones (seven days during probation, art. 335b para. 1; one month in the first year of service, two months in the second to ninth years, three months thereafter)? Not: A notice period that only the termination letter states, with no written contract term behind it. (art. 335c para. 2)

### Stops

- If `employer_gave_notice` is no, the process ends. The provision restrains only a termination by the employer; where the employer gave no notice there is nothing it can make void or suspend, so this process ends. (art. 336c para. 1, introduction)
- If `employer_gave_notice` is yes and `notice_during_probation` is yes, the process ends. The bar applies only after the probation period, extended under art. 335b para. 3 where it was interrupted, has expired; notice given during it is outside the bar, so this process ends. (art. 336c para. 1, introduction)

### Steps and documents

1. Establish whether the employer gave notice. Taken when: always. (art. 336c para. 1, introduction)
2. Record the notice, the start of employment and the probation terms, and check that the probation period had expired when notice was given. Taken when: `employer_gave_notice` is yes. (art. 336c para. 1, introduction)
   - Notice of termination (letter, message or other record of the notice and its receipt), showing: issuer; addressee; date of the notice; date the employee received it; stated end of employment. Needed always. (art. 336c para. 2: Voidness turns on the day the notice was given compared with the protected period, and the same day decides whether notice fell in probation; the notice and its date of receipt are the ordinary record of that day (the cited text sets no written form, so a message or other record serves).)
   - Employment contract or other record of the start of employment, showing: parties; start date of employment; probation clause, if any; notice-period clause, if any. Needed always. (art. 335b para. 1: The probation period runs from the start of employment, and the year of service sets the 30, 90 or 180 days of art. 336c para. 1 let. b; the contract or another record of the start date ordinarily shows it.)
   - Written probation clause (individual agreement, standard or collective contract), showing: length of the probation period; the written instrument that sets it. Needed only when `probation_varied` is yes. (art. 335b para. 2: A probation period other than the first month exists only under a written agreement or a standard or collective contract; that written clause proves its length and so whether notice came during it.)
3. Examine the illness or accident protected period. Taken when: (`employer_gave_notice` is yes and `notice_during_probation` is no) and `incapacity_illness_accident` is yes. (art. 336c para. 1 let. b)
   - Proof of the hindrance from working (medical certificate, or official order or summons for a non-voluntary legal obligation), showing: medical certificate; cause (illness or accident); full or partial incapacity; first and last day of incapacity; date issued. Needed always. (art. 336c para. 1 let. b: The bar applies only while the employee is prevented from working by illness or accident, for at most 30, 90 or 180 days; a medical certificate is the ordinary proof of the incapacity and its dates, which with the start date of employment fix that period.)
4. Examine the compulsory-service protected period. Taken when: (`employer_gave_notice` is yes and `notice_during_probation` is no) and `compulsory_service` is yes. (art. 336c para. 1 let. a)
   - Official confirmation of compulsory service, showing: kind of service (military, civil defence, alternative civilian); first and last day of service. Needed always. (art. 336c para. 1 let. a: The bar depends on the kind of service, its dates and whether it lasted more than eleven days; the official service order or confirmation ordinarily shows all three.)
5. Examine the pregnancy and maternity protected period. Taken when: (`employer_gave_notice` is yes and `notice_during_probation` is no) and `pregnancy_or_birth` is yes. (art. 336c para. 1 let. c)
   - Medical confirmation of pregnancy or record of the birth, showing: pregnancy confirmed and its dates, or the date of birth. Needed always. (art. 336c para. 1 let. c: The bar runs during pregnancy and sixteen weeks after the birth; a medical confirmation of the pregnancy or the birth record ordinarily dates that period.)
6. Compute the suspended and extended notice period. Taken when: (`employer_gave_notice` is yes and `notice_during_probation` is no) and (`incapacity_illness_accident` is yes or `compulsory_service` is yes or `pregnancy_or_birth` is yes) and `notice_before_protected_period` is yes. (art. 336c para. 2)
   - Written notice-period clause (individual, standard or collective contract), showing: notice period length; end-point of the notice (e.g. end of a month); the written instrument that sets it (individual, standard or collective contract). Needed only when `notice_period_varied` is yes. (art. 335c para. 2: A notice period other than the statutory one exists only under a written contract; resuming and extending a suspended notice period (art. 336c para. 3) needs its length and end-point, which that clause ordinarily shows.)
7. Check whether the notice fell inside a protected period and is void. Taken when: (`employer_gave_notice` is yes and `notice_during_probation` is no) and (`incapacity_illness_accident` is yes or `compulsory_service` is yes or `pregnancy_or_birth` is yes) and `notice_before_protected_period` is no. (art. 336c para. 2)
8. Assess the effect of the notice (void, suspended or valid). Taken when: `employer_gave_notice` is yes and `notice_during_probation` is no. (art. 336c para. 2)

### Presumptions

- No article states what an unmentioned fact is. Under the convention of this benchmark (not law), not mentioned means taken as no: `notice_during_probation`, `probation_varied`, `incapacity_illness_accident`, `compulsory_service`, `pregnancy_or_birth`, `notice_period_varied`.
- No presumption; always stated in the file: `notice_before_protected_period`.
- The defining event `employer_gave_notice` is never presumed.

## Type 2: Wrongful (abusive) notice of termination by the employer: objection and compensation claim (CO arts. 336-336b)

Notice is abusive ("missbräuchlich"; the English translation says "unlawful") when given for one of the reasons in art. 336; an abusive notice still ends the employment relationship and gives rise to compensation, set by the court and capped at six months' salary (art. 336a para. 2). Only two reasons are covered here: retaliation for claims asserted in good faith (art. 336 para. 1 let. d) and notice to an elected employee representative where the employer cannot prove a justified reason ("begründeter Anlass", art. 336 para. 2 let. b). To claim compensation, the employee must lodge a written objection with the employer no later than the end of the notice period (art. 336b para. 1); the claim exists only where the objection was validly made and the parties did not agree to continue the relationship (art. 336b para. 2), and it is forfeited ("verwirkt"; the English says "prescribes") unless an action is made pending ("anhängig gemacht") within 180 days of the end of the employment relationship (art. 336b para. 2). Read with the Civil Procedure Code (CPC) art. 62 para. 1, a case becomes pending when a conciliation request or an action is filed (an inference across the two acts: the CO says "anhängig gemacht", the CPC defines "Rechtshängigkeit"). The party giving notice must state the reasons in writing if the other party asks (art. 335 para. 2).

Defining event: `employer_gave_notice`. It is never presumed.

### Conditions

1. `employer_gave_notice`: Did the employer give the employee notice of termination of the employment relationship? Not: A resignation by the employee, a fixed-term relationship that ended by running out, or a dismissal with immediate effect (art. 337, a separate type). (art. 336b para. 2)
2. `notice_period_ended`: Has the notice period (from the notice to the end of the employment relationship) already ended? Not: The employee's last working day where they were released from work while the notice period was still running; the end date stated in the notice where a protected period has suspended the notice period (art. 336c para. 2) or untaken leave of the other parent has extended it (art. 335c para. 3): the period then ends later, and if the file raises either, the fact is open. (art. 336b para. 1) Always stated in the file (stated or open, never left unmentioned).
3. `objection_submitted`: Did the employee lodge a written objection to the notice with the employer (German: "beim Kündigenden schriftlich Einsprache erheben") no later than the end of the notice period? Not: An oral protest; an objection addressed to someone other than the employer; a written objection lodged only after the notice period had ended. (art. 336b para. 1)
4. `agreed_to_continue`: Did the employee and the employer agree, after the notice, that the employment relationship would continue? Not: Talks about continuing that ended without agreement; an agreement only about a payment or about the end date. (art. 336b para. 2)
5. `more_than_180_days`: Have more than 180 days passed since the employment relationship ended? Not: 180 days counted from the notice or from the last day actually worked rather than from the end of the employment relationship; an end date that a protected period (art. 336c para. 2) or untaken leave of the other parent (art. 335c para. 3) has moved later: if the file raises either, the fact is open. (art. 336b para. 2) Always stated in the file (stated or open, never left unmentioned).
6. `action_in_time`: Did the employee make the compensation claim pending (German: "Klage anhängig gemacht") within 180 days of the end of the employment relationship? Not: A demand letter to the employer; a claim made pending only after the 180 days. (art. 336b para. 2)
7. `reasons_requested`: Did the employee ask the employer to state the reasons for the notice in writing? Not: Reasons the employer gave without being asked, or a request made only orally to a colleague rather than to the employer. (art. 335 para. 2)
8. `retaliation_for_claims`: Was the notice given because the employee had asserted, in good faith, claims arising from the employment relationship? The employee's own account of the employer's reason counts as stating it, also where the employee gives it as a conviction or belief; it is open only where the claim itself says the reason is unknown, uncertain or one of several possibilities. Not: Complaints that are not claims under the employment relationship, or a mere coincidence in time with no link to the notice. (art. 336 para. 1 let. d)
9. `employee_representative`: Was the employee, when notice was given, an elected employee representative on the staff council of the business or on a body linked to the business? Not: Trade-union membership or union activity without an elected mandate (art. 336 para. 2 let. a, not covered here); a representative whose mandate ended through a transfer of the employment relationship, whose protection continues (art. 336 para. 3, not covered here). (art. 336 para. 2 let. b)

### Stops

- If `employer_gave_notice` is no, the process ends. Only the party on whom notice was served may bring the compensation claim; where the employer gave no notice there is no such claim, so this process ends. (art. 336b para. 2)
- If `employer_gave_notice` is yes and (`notice_period_ended` is yes and `objection_submitted` is no), the process ends. The party served may claim only where the objection has been validly ("gültig") made, which para. 1 requires in writing to the employer by the end of the notice period; once that period has ended without it, the claim cannot be brought, so this process ends. (art. 336b para. 2)
- If `employer_gave_notice` is yes and not (`notice_period_ended` is yes and `objection_submitted` is no) and `agreed_to_continue` is yes, the process ends. The party served may claim only where the parties cannot reach agreement on continuing the employment relationship; where they agreed to continue it, there is no claim, so this process ends. (art. 336b para. 2)
- If (`employer_gave_notice` is yes and not (`notice_period_ended` is yes and `objection_submitted` is no) and `agreed_to_continue` is no) and (`more_than_180_days` is yes and `action_in_time` is no), the process ends. The German text, which is authoritative, says the claim is forfeited ("verwirkt") unless an action is made pending ("anhängig gemacht") within 180 days; forfeiture applies by itself, unlike the English "prescribes", so the process ends. (art. 336b para. 2)

### Steps and documents

1. Establish whether the employer gave notice. Taken when: always. (art. 336b para. 2)
2. Record the notice and the notice period, and check the written objection. Taken when: `employer_gave_notice` is yes. (art. 336b para. 1)
   - Notice of termination (letter, message or other record of the notice and its receipt), showing: issuer; addressee; date of the notice; date the employee received it; stated end of employment. Needed always. (art. 336b para. 1: The objection must be lodged before the end of the notice period; the notice and its date of receipt ordinarily show when the period began and when the employment ends, which fixes that window and the start of the 180 days.)
   - Employment contract or other record of the start of employment, showing: parties; start date of employment; probation clause, if any; notice-period clause, if any. Needed always. (art. 335c para. 1: The end of the notice period, which closes the objection window, depends on the years of service and on any written variation; the contract ordinarily shows the start date and any such clause.)
   - Copy of the written objection with proof of when the employer received it, showing: objection to the notice; addressed to the employer; date lodged or received; proof of sending or receipt. Needed only when `objection_submitted` is yes. (art. 336b para. 1: The claim requires a written objection lodged with the employer before the notice period ends; a copy with proof of its date is the ordinary proof of both.)
3. Check whether the parties agreed to continue the employment relationship. Taken when: `employer_gave_notice` is yes and not (`notice_period_ended` is yes and `objection_submitted` is no). (art. 336b para. 2)
4. Check the 180-day time limit for making the claim pending. Taken when: `employer_gave_notice` is yes and not (`notice_period_ended` is yes and `objection_submitted` is no) and `agreed_to_continue` is no. (art. 336b para. 2)
   - Proof that the claim was made pending: the conciliation request or statement of claim, with the confirmation of receipt, showing: authority (conciliation authority or court); compensation claim; filing date; confirmation of receipt. Needed only when `action_in_time` is yes. (CPC art. 62 para. 1: Art. 336b para. 2 (German) requires the action to be made pending ("anhängig gemacht") within 180 days; under CPC art. 62 para. 1 a case becomes pending when an application for conciliation or an action is filed, and para. 2 provides a confirmation of receipt, so that submission with its date proves it (the link from the CO's "anhängig gemacht" to the CPC's "Rechtshängigkeit" is an inference across the two acts).)
5. Establish the reason the employer gave for the notice. Taken when: `employer_gave_notice` is yes and not (`notice_period_ended` is yes and `objection_submitted` is no) and `agreed_to_continue` is no and not (`more_than_180_days` is yes and `action_in_time` is no). (art. 336 para. 1, introduction)
   - Employer's written statement of reasons, showing: reasons stated by the employer; date. Needed only when `reasons_requested` is yes. (art. 335 para. 2: Where the employee asked, the employer must give its reasons in writing; that statement is the ordinary record of the reason on which art. 336 makes abuse depend.)
6. Examine the claimed retaliation for asserting claims. Taken when: (`employer_gave_notice` is yes and not (`notice_period_ended` is yes and `objection_submitted` is no) and `agreed_to_continue` is no and not (`more_than_180_days` is yes and `action_in_time` is no)) and `retaliation_for_claims` is yes. (art. 336 para. 1 let. d)
   - Correspondence in which the employee asserted claims before the notice, showing: claims asserted under the employment relationship; dates before the notice; employer's response, if any. Needed always. (art. 336 para. 1 let. d: This ground turns on claims asserted before the notice; the correspondence in which the employee raised them is the ordinary record of what was claimed and when.)
7. Examine the protection of an elected employee representative (the employer must prove a justified reason, "begründeter Anlass"). Taken when: (`employer_gave_notice` is yes and not (`notice_period_ended` is yes and `objection_submitted` is no) and `agreed_to_continue` is no and not (`more_than_180_days` is yes and `action_in_time` is no)) and `employee_representative` is yes. (art. 336 para. 2 let. b)
   - Proof of election as employee representative, showing: body (staff council or body linked to the business); election; term of the mandate covering the notice date. Needed always. (art. 336 para. 2 let. b: The protection applies while the employee is an elected representative; the election record or mandate confirmation ordinarily shows the body and the term.)
8. Assess the compensation claim. Taken when: `employer_gave_notice` is yes and not (`notice_period_ended` is yes and `objection_submitted` is no) and `agreed_to_continue` is no and not (`more_than_180_days` is yes and `action_in_time` is no). (art. 336a para. 2)
   - Salary statements (pay slips), showing: monthly salary; period covered. Needed always. (art. 336a para. 2: Compensation is capped at six months' salary; pay slips are the ordinary proof of the salary that sets the cap.)

### Presumptions

- No article states what an unmentioned fact is. Under the convention of this benchmark (not law), not mentioned means taken as no: `agreed_to_continue`, `reasons_requested`, `retaliation_for_claims`, `employee_representative`.
- Not mentioned leaves the fact unresolved, because its absence would end the process: `action_in_time`, `objection_submitted`.
- No presumption; always stated in the file: `notice_period_ended`, `more_than_180_days`.
- The defining event `employer_gave_notice` is never presumed.

## Type 3: Dismissal with immediate effect by the employer (CO arts. 337, 337c)

Either party may end the employment relationship with immediate effect at any time for good cause ("wichtige Gründe") and must give the reasons in writing if the other party asks (art. 337 para. 1). Good cause is any circumstance that makes continuing the relationship in good faith unconscionable for the party giving notice (art. 337 para. 2). Being prevented from working through no fault of one's own ("unverschuldete Verhinderung") is never good cause (art. 337 para. 3). Where the employer dismisses with immediate effect without good cause, the employee is entitled to what they would have earned had the relationship ended after the required notice period or at the end of its agreed term (art. 337c para. 1), reduced among other things by what they earned from other work (art. 337c para. 2; only earnings from other work are covered here); the court may add compensation of up to six months' salary (art. 337c para. 3).

Defining event: `dismissed_immediately`. It is never presumed.

### Conditions

1. `dismissed_immediately`: Did the employer end the employment relationship with immediate effect, without a notice period? Not: Ordinary notice with a notice period, however short, or release from work while a notice period runs. (art. 337c para. 1)
2. `reasons_requested`: Did the employee ask the employer to state the reasons for the immediate dismissal in writing? Not: Reasons the employer gave without being asked. (art. 337 para. 1)
3. `incapacity_cause`: Does the employer give as the cause of the dismissal the employee's absence from work, and does the employee say they were prevented from working through no fault of their own (for instance illness, accident or a non-voluntary legal obligation)? Not: A dismissal for conduct at work, or an absence the employee does not say was without their fault. (art. 337 para. 3)
4. `fixed_term`: Was the employment relationship agreed for a fixed term, with an agreed end date, rather than for an unlimited period? Not: An open-ended contract that only states a probation period or a notice period. (art. 337c para. 1) Always stated in the file (stated or open, never left unmentioned).
5. `notice_period_varied`: Does the file say that a written individual employment contract, a standard employment contract or a collective employment contract sets notice periods different from the statutory ones (seven days during probation, art. 335b para. 1; one month in the first year of service, two months in the second to ninth years, three months thereafter)? Not: A notice period that only the termination letter states, with no written contract term behind it. (art. 335c para. 2)
6. `other_earnings`: Has the employee earned income from other work during the time the notice period or the agreed term would still have run? Not: Unemployment benefits; savings resulting from the termination and work intentionally forgone (also deductible under para. 2, not covered here). (art. 337c para. 2)

### Stops

- If `dismissed_immediately` is no, the process ends. The entitlement arises only where the employer dismisses with immediate effect; without such a dismissal this process has nothing to assess, so it ends. (art. 337c para. 1)

### Steps and documents

1. Establish whether the employer dismissed with immediate effect. Taken when: always. (art. 337c para. 1)
2. Record the dismissal, the contract and the salary. Taken when: `dismissed_immediately` is yes. (art. 337c para. 1)
   - Letter or message dismissing with immediate effect, showing: issuer; addressee; date; that employment ends with immediate effect. Needed always. (art. 337c para. 1: The entitlement arises from a dismissal with immediate effect; the dismissal letter or message is the ordinary record that it was immediate and of its date.)
   - Employment contract or other record of its terms, showing: parties; start date of employment; agreed end date, if fixed-term; probation clause, if any; notice-period clause, if any. Needed always. (art. 337c para. 1: Damages run to the end of the required notice period or of the agreed term; the contract ordinarily shows the start date, any agreed end date, any probation clause and any notice clause.)
   - Salary statements (pay slips), showing: monthly salary; period covered. Needed always. (art. 337c para. 1: Damages equal what the employee would have earned; pay slips are the ordinary proof of those earnings (and of the salary that caps compensation under para. 3).)
3. Establish the cause the employer relies on. Taken when: `dismissed_immediately` is yes. (art. 337 para. 2)
   - Employer's written statement of reasons, showing: reasons stated by the employer; date. Needed only when `reasons_requested` is yes. (art. 337 para. 1: Where the employee asked, the employer must give its reasons in writing; that statement is the ordinary record of the cause it relies on.)
   - Proof of the hindrance from working (medical certificate, or official order or summons for a non-voluntary legal obligation), showing: cause of the hindrance; first and last day; date issued. Needed only when `incapacity_cause` is yes. (art. 337 para. 3: Being prevented from working through no fault of one's own can never be good cause; a medical certificate, or the official order or summons for a legal obligation, is the ordinary proof of the hindrance and its dates.)
4. Measure damages to the agreed end of the fixed term. Taken when: `dismissed_immediately` is yes and `fixed_term` is yes. (art. 337c para. 1)
5. Measure damages to the end of the required notice period. Taken when: `dismissed_immediately` is yes and `fixed_term` is no. (art. 337c para. 1)
   - Written notice-period clause (individual, standard or collective contract), showing: notice period length; end-point of the notice (e.g. end of a month); the written instrument that sets it (individual, standard or collective contract). Needed only when `notice_period_varied` is yes. (art. 335c para. 2: Damages run to the end of the required notice period; where a written contract varies the statutory periods, that clause is the ordinary proof of the period that applies.)
6. Assess damages and compensation. Taken when: `dismissed_immediately` is yes. (art. 337c para. 3)
   - Proof of earnings from other work since the dismissal, showing: employer or client; period; amounts earned. Needed only when `other_earnings` is yes. (art. 337c para. 2: Damages are reduced by what the employee earned from other work; pay slips or contracts for that work are the ordinary proof of the amounts and period.)

### Presumptions

- No article states what an unmentioned fact is. Under the convention of this benchmark (not law), not mentioned means taken as no: `reasons_requested`, `incapacity_cause`, `notice_period_varied`, `other_earnings`.
- No presumption; always stated in the file: `fixed_term`.
- The defining event `dismissed_immediately` is never presumed.
