# Theft rule set from public text (Study 4, step 2a)

Version 2026-11-12. Generated by `tools/build.py`; `rules.json` is the same content. Scope: claims under a CSS household-contents policy (AVB Ausgabe 02.2024), with the Insurance Contract Act (VVG, SR 221.229.1, in force since 1 January 2024). One insurer's conditions only, so that no clause is applied outside its own contract. Development material, not a result.

Basis: **states** = the passage states the rule; **follows** = it follows by the step in 'inference'; **evidence** = the passage creates the condition and the rule names the document that ordinarily proves it. *mandatory: false* = the passage makes the document due only when CSS asks for it. 'Context' passages are further verbatim quotations the clause relies on (definitions, exclusions, limits).

## Convention of this benchmark (not law)

> A fact that the file does not mention is taken as not alleged, and the rules are applied as if it were absent. Silence never ends a process: where a fact's absence would end it, the unmentioned fact is unresolved. The defining event is never presumed: if the file does not mention it, it is unresolved. A fact that the file raises and leaves unsettled is open.

Rule `intake_convention`, basis `convention`, source `benchmark-convention` (SPEC.md items 6a, 6b). No passage supports a presumption in this rule set, so `presumptions` is empty in both types. The workflows encode the convention as an 'unstated' presumption (value false) on every branch fact except the defining events and the two facts marked `always_stated`: `burglary_or_robbery` (within a theft at home, burglary or robbery and simple theft are both ordinary) and `away_cover_missing` (the supplementary cover is an option, 'sofern ... gewünscht und in der Police aufgeführt', Ziff. 24 Einleitung; the policy settles it). No fact's value false triggers a stop besides the defining events; the two stops on a branch fact (household member, missing supplementary cover) are reached only when the fact is true. Polarity (6b): every branch fact is worded so that true is the circumstance a party would raise; `imminent_danger` is the exception a claimant raises against the instruction duty.

## Open points of the earlier reviews, settled from the words or left out

- **Third party (old rule 042; Study 3 condition g042).** Left out. In the CSS conditions the only third-party clause is Ziff. 15: "Sofern die CSS aus diesem Vertrag Leistungen erbracht hat, für welche der Versicherungsnehmer gegenüber Dritten Ansprüche geltend machen könnte, hat der Versicherungsnehmer diese Ansprüche bis zur Höhe der erbrachten Leistungen an die CSS abzutreten." Its condition is that CSS has already paid, which an intake file never shows, and its consequence is an assignment, not a document. The passage does not say whether an unidentified thief is a third party against whom claims 'could' be asserted, so no condition may turn on identification. The claim form asks "Wer hat den Schaden verursacht?" (Abschnitt 3.5): a question on the form, answerable with 'unknown'; it names no document. Generali's recourse clause (old rule 042's second limb) belongs to another insurer's contract and is not used.
- **Traces (old rules 003 and 005; Study 3 condition g003).** Two different clauses use the word. (a) Ziff. 23.3 covers damage "durch Spuren, Zeugen oder nach den Umständen schlüssig nachgewiesen": traces are one of three alternative means of proof, and the circumstances alone can suffice, so no document and no condition turns on whether traces exist; the defining event is the event, not its proof. (b) Ziff. 8 lit. f forbids removing or altering traces without the police's consent: the condition is removal or alteration (done or intended), never the mere existence of traces (fact traces_changed).
- **Consents (old rule 003, D26/D27).** Settled from the words for the police's consent: Ziff. 8 lit. f requires it for any removal or alteration of traces and states no exception, so the mitigation and public-interest exceptions of other clauses are not read into it (document police_consent). The insurer's consent to changes of damaged items (VVG Art. 38b) and the 'help services' arm (Simpego only) are left out: the insurer's consent is the insurer's own act, not a document a claimant supplies; CSS Ziff. 8 lit. e forbids such changes without naming any consent; and the Simpego text is another insurer's.
- **Insurer's instruction (old rule 004.a, D25).** Settled from the words of VVG Art. 38a Abs. 1: the duty is to obtain the insurer's instruction "über die zu ergreifenden Massnahmen", "wenn nicht Gefahr im Verzug liegt". It concerns measures to reduce the loss that are still to be taken, does not arise from the notification itself, and lapses under imminent danger. Two facts carry it: measures_pending (true = measures still to be taken) and imminent_danger (true = the exception a claimant would raise; unmentioned = none). The step h_instruction / a_instruction is reached when measures are pending and no danger is raised. No document is attached: the instruction is the insurer's own (the old D25 is not used).
- **Police report (old rules 002, 049).** Settled: CSS Ziff. 8 lit. f makes notifying the police a duty on every theft but says the report "kann durch die CSS einverlangt werden", and no CSS document requests it at intake (the claim form asks only for the report's particulars in section 4: Polizeimeldung, Anzeigedatum, Polizeiposten). So the handler does not request it at intake (mandatory: false; see the reading of optional documents).
- **Notification record (old rules 001, 047).** The duty in VVG Art. 38 Abs. 1 is to notify; CSS requires no form (Ziff. 4: "Wird bei einer Mitteilung kein Formerfordernis angeführt, kann diese auch mündlich erfolgen.") and its form offers online notification. The document is therefore any record of the notification (notification_record), with no signature item.
- **Valued list and original vouchers (old rules 013, 014).** Both rest on the same contract clause, Ziff. 8 lit. c ("auf Verlangen, ein Verzeichnis ... mit Wertangaben zu erstellen und Originalbelege beizubringen"), the clause VVG Art. 39 Abs. 2 Ziff. 1 refers to, and are treated alike. The handler is the insurer, so the request is the act being decided; CSS makes it in its own claim form for every stolen or damaged item (section 3.7, with its columns, 'bitte Originalkaufbelege beilegen', or a separate list). At intake both are therefore requested (mandatory: true). The cost limit of Art. 39 applies voucher by voucher: original_vouchers must show the vouchers that can be obtained without considerable cost, and the whole need falls away only when the file states that none can.

## Left out (no passage, no document, another insurer, or beyond the size)

- Insurer's consent to changing damaged items (VVG Art. 38b; CSS Ziff. 8 lit. e): the consent is the insurer's own act, not a claimant document; CSS names no consent.
- The claim form's signature and declaration: not required by the notification duty (VVG Art. 38 Abs. 1; CSS Ziff. 4 allows oral notice).
- Recovered items: notify CSS (Ziff. 8 lit. h), repay compensation (Ziff. 29 lit. e): a step without a document that changes nothing in the intake checklist; repayment arises only after payment.
- Measures to find the thief (Ziff. 8 lit. g): a duty "nach Anleitung der Polizei oder der CSS" with no document.
- Third-party claims (Ziff. 15): applies only after CSS has paid (see the settled points).
- SIM-card misuse (Ziff. 24.5): the two exclusion sentences name no document; the old rule's bank statement had no passage.
- Jewellery and watch limits, safe storage (Ziff. 23.3 lit. d): limits of the amount, no document named; the watch sentence is broken across a page in the PDF.
- Alarm system and deductible (Ziff. 28 lit. e): affects the deductible only; no document named.
