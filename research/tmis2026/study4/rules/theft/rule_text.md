# Theft claims under CSS household-contents insurance: rules

These rules apply to claims for theft of household contents under a CSS household-contents policy (AVB Ausgabe 02.2024) and the Swiss Insurance Contract Act (VVG). Each rule quotes the passage it rests on. A fact is answered true, false, open (the file raises it and leaves it unsettled) or not stated.

## Convention of this benchmark (not law)

The following rule is a convention of this benchmark, not of the law; no article states it:

> A fact that the file does not mention is taken as not alleged, and the rules are applied as if it were absent. Silence never ends a process: where a fact's absence would end it, the unmentioned fact is unresolved. The defining event is never presumed: if the file does not mention it, it is unresolved. A fact that the file raises and leaves unsettled is open.

## Type 1: Theft at the insured location (CSS AVB Ziff. 23.3)

Defining event: Does the file state that household contents at the insured location (the Versicherungsort of the policy) were stolen or damaged by a burglary, a robbery or a simple theft (including vandalism), even if nothing was taken?

### Facts

- **theft_at_home**: Does the file state that household contents at the insured location (the Versicherungsort of the policy) were stolen or damaged by a burglary, a robbery or a simple theft (including vandalism), even if nothing was taken? Not this: Items lost, misplaced or misappropriated; a theft away from the insured location (the other claim type, Ziff. 24.2). Source: css-avb Ziff. 23.3 Abs. 1, S. 7: «Schäden am Hausrat, auch wenn dabei nichts entwendet wurde, durch Einbruch, Beraubung und einfacher Diebstahl (inkl. Vandalismus) am Versicherungsort.»
- **household_perpetrator**: Does the file state that the theft or damage was caused by a person who lives in the same household as the policyholder? Not this: A guest, neighbour, tradesman or cleaner, or a former household member who no longer lives there; a suspicion the file raises but does not settle is open, not true. Source: css-avb Ziff. 23.3, Ausschlüsse, S. 7: «Schäden, verursacht durch Personen, die mit dem Versicherungsnehmer im gleichen Haushalt leben.»
- **burglary_or_robbery** (always stated or open in a file): Was the theft a burglary (the thief forcibly entered the building or a room of it, or broke open a container in it, or unlocked it with the correct keys or codes that the thief had obtained by burglary or robbery) or a robbery (theft under threat or use of violence against the policyholder or a member of the household, or while they could not resist because of unconsciousness, accident or death), and so not a simple theft? Not this: A theft through a door or window left open or unlocked, without force and without violence against a person: that is a simple theft. Source: css-avb Ziff. 23.3 lit. c, S. 7: «Einfacher Diebstahl am Versicherungsort ist Diebstahl, der weder als Einbruchdiebstahl noch als Beraubung gilt.»
- **lock_change**: Does the claim include costs of changing or replacing keys, magnetic cards or locks at the location named in the policy, or at a bank safe the policyholder rents? Not this: Emergency doors, glazing or locks put in after the event; the repair of a damaged door or window. Source: css-avb Ziff. 20 lit. d, S. 5: «Mitversichert sind die effektiven Kosten für das Ändern oder Ersetzen von Schlüsseln, Magnetkarten oder von Schlössern an dem in der Police bezeichneten Standort und an den vom Versicherungsnehmer gemieteten Banksafes und dazugehörenden Schlüsseln.»
- **traces_changed**: Does the file state that traces of the theft (for example pry marks, a broken lock, window or container) have been removed or altered, or are to be removed or altered, for example by repair or cleaning? Not this: Traces that exist and are left as they were, or were only photographed; the police examining the scene. Source: css-avb Ziff. 8 lit. f, S. 3: «ohne Zustimmung der Polizei die Tatspuren nicht zu entfernen oder zu verändern»
- **bicycle**: Is a bicycle among the stolen or damaged items? Not this: A motor scooter or motorcycle; bicycle parts or accessories alone. Source: css-form Abschnitt 3.9, S. 3: «Fahrrad (bitte Originalkaufbelege beilegen)»
- **vouchers_unobtainable**: Does the file state that the original purchase vouchers for the stolen or damaged items cannot be obtained, or only at considerable cost (not even as a duplicate from the seller)? Not this: The vouchers have not been attached yet or not been looked for; finding them is merely inconvenient. Source: vvg VVG Art. 39 Abs. 2 Ziff. 1: «Der Vertrag kann verfügen: 1. dass der Anspruchsberechtigte bestimmte Belege, deren Beschaffung ihm ohne erhebliche Kosten möglich ist, insbesondere auch ärztliche Bescheinigungen, beizubringen hat;»
- **instruction_due**: Does the file state that measures to reduce the loss are or were to be taken after the theft, and that there was no imminent danger (Gefahr im Verzug) requiring them before the insurer could be asked? Not this: Measures that had to be taken at once because of imminent danger; the notification of the claim itself; steps to find the thief or recover the items. Source: vvg VVG Art. 38a Abs. 1: «Der Anspruchsberechtigte ist verpflichtet, nach Eintritt des befürchteten Ereignisses tunlichst für Minderung des Schadens zu sorgen. Er muss, wenn nicht Gefahr im Verzug liegt, über die zu ergreifenden Massnahmen die Weisung des Versicherungsunternehmens einholen und befolgen.»

### Stops

- Not a theft at the insured location: this claim type does not apply: when theft_at_home = false. Source: Ziff. 23.3 Abs. 1, S. 7: «Schäden am Hausrat, auch wenn dabei nichts entwendet wurde, durch Einbruch, Beraubung und einfacher Diebstahl (inkl. Vandalismus) am Versicherungsort.» (The clause covers damage by these events at the insured location; where the file states that no such event occurred, the steps of this claim type do not apply.)
- Excluded: caused by a person living in the policyholder's household: when theft_at_home = true and household_perpetrator = true. Source: Ziff. 23.3, Ausschlüsse, S. 7: «Schäden, verursacht durch Personen, die mit dem Versicherungsnehmer im gleichen Haushalt leben.»

### Steps, in order

- **Check that the reported event is a theft at the insured location** (h_event), reached when always. Source: Ziff. 23.3 Abs. 1, S. 7: «Schäden am Hausrat, auch wenn dabei nichts entwendet wurde, durch Einbruch, Beraubung und einfacher Diebstahl (inkl. Vandalismus) am Versicherungsort.»
- **Check the exclusion of damage caused by household members** (h_exclusion), reached when theft_at_home = true. Source: Ziff. 23.3, Ausschlüsse, S. 7: «Schäden, verursacht durch Personen, die mit dem Versicherungsnehmer im gleichen Haushalt leben.»
- **Record the claim notification and the policy** (h_notify), reached when theft_at_home = true and household_perpetrator = false. Source: VVG Art. 38 Abs. 1: «Ist das befürchtete Ereignis eingetreten, so muss der Anspruchsberechtigte, sobald er von diesem Ereignisse und seinem Anspruche aus der Versicherung Kenntnis erlangt, das Versicherungsunternehmen benachrichtigen.»
- **Check that the police were notified and an official investigation requested** (h_police), reached when theft_at_home = true and household_perpetrator = false. Source: Ziff. 8 lit. f, S. 3: «die Polizeibehörde umgehend zu benachrichtigen, eine amtliche Untersuchung zu beantragen»
- **Check the police's consent to removing or altering the traces** (h_traces), reached when theft_at_home = true and household_perpetrator = false and traces_changed = true. Source: Ziff. 8 lit. f, S. 3: «ohne Zustimmung der Polizei die Tatspuren nicht zu entfernen oder zu verändern»
- **Check whether the insurer's instruction on loss-reduction measures is to be obtained** (h_instruction_check), reached when theft_at_home = true and household_perpetrator = false. Source: VVG Art. 38a Abs. 1 Satz 2: «Er muss, wenn nicht Gefahr im Verzug liegt, über die zu ergreifenden Massnahmen die Weisung des Versicherungsunternehmens einholen und befolgen.»
- **Obtain and follow the insurer's instruction on the measures to be taken** (h_instruction), reached when theft_at_home = true and household_perpetrator = false and instruction_due = true. Source: VVG Art. 38a Abs. 1 Satz 2: «Er muss, wenn nicht Gefahr im Verzug liegt, über die zu ergreifenden Massnahmen die Weisung des Versicherungsunternehmens einholen und befolgen.»
- **Establish the stolen or damaged items and their value** (h_loss), reached when theft_at_home = true and household_perpetrator = false. Source: Ziff. 29 lit. a, S. 11: «Der Versicherungsnehmer muss die Schadenhöhe beweisen.»

### Documents

- **Claim notification: the CSS form 'Schadenanzeige Hausrat / Gebäude', completed and signed** (claim_form): step h_notify; needed when always; mandatory; must show: that and when CSS was notified of the theft; the signature under the declaration that all questions were answered truthfully and completely. Source: VVG Art. 38 Abs. 1: «Ist das befürchtete Ereignis eingetreten, so muss der Anspruchsberechtigte, sobald er von diesem Ereignisse und seinem Anspruche aus der Versicherung Kenntnis erlangt, das Versicherungsunternehmen benachrichtigen.» (The passage makes notifying the insurer a duty; the insurer's own claim form, completed and signed, is the document that ordinarily records that and when the insurer was notified.)
- **The insurance policy (Police)** (insurance_policy): step h_notify; needed when always; mandatory; must show: theft (Ziff. 23.3) among the insured perils; the insured location. Source: Ziff. 23, Einleitung, S. 5: «Die CSS versichert die nachfolgend beschriebenen Gefahren und Schäden, sofern diese vom Versicherungsnehmer gewünscht und in der Police aufgeführt worden sind:» (The clause makes cover depend on the peril being listed in the policy; the policy is the document that shows whether theft is listed and which location is insured.)
- **Police report on the theft (Polizeirapport)** (police_report): step h_police; needed when always; only if CSS asks for it (not mandatory); must show: that the theft was reported to the police; that an official investigation was requested. Source: Ziff. 8 lit. f, S. 3: «Ein entsprechender Polizeirapport kann durch die CSS einverlangt werden»
- **The police's consent to removing or altering the traces (a written confirmation, or the entry in the police report recording it)** (police_consent): step h_traces; needed when always; mandatory; must show: that the police consented; which traces the consent covered. Source: Ziff. 8 lit. f, S. 3: «ohne Zustimmung der Polizei die Tatspuren nicht zu entfernen oder zu verändern» (The passage makes the police's consent the condition for removing or altering traces; once traces have been or are to be removed or altered, the police's written confirmation of that consent is the document that ordinarily shows the condition was met.)
- **List of the stolen or damaged items with their values (Verzeichnis mit Wertangaben)** (stolen_items_list): step h_loss; needed when always; only if CSS asks for it (not mandatory); must show: each stolen or damaged item; its value. Source: Ziff. 8 lit. c, S. 3: «auf Verlangen, ein Verzeichnis der vom Schaden betroffenen Sachen mit Wertangaben zu erstellen und Originalbelege beizubringen;»
- **Original purchase vouchers for the stolen or damaged items (Originalkaufbelege)** (original_vouchers): step h_loss; needed when vouchers_unobtainable = false; mandatory; must show: the item; where and when it was bought; the price paid. Source: Abschnitt 3.7 Gegenstände, S. 2: «Beschädigte oder gestohlene Sachen (bitte Originalkaufbelege beilegen)»
- **Original purchase voucher for the bicycle** (bicycle_voucher): step h_loss; needed when bicycle = true and vouchers_unobtainable = false; mandatory; must show: the bicycle; where and when it was bought; the price paid. Source: Abschnitt 3.9, S. 3: «Fahrrad (bitte Originalkaufbelege beilegen)»
- **Invoice for changing or replacing the keys, magnetic cards or locks** (lock_change_invoice): step h_loss; needed when lock_change = true and burglary_or_robbery = true; mandatory; must show: the effective cost; which keys, cards or locks were changed, and where. Source: Ziff. 20 lit. d, S. 5: «Mitversichert sind die effektiven Kosten für das Ändern oder Ersetzen von Schlüsseln, Magnetkarten oder von Schlössern an dem in der Police bezeichneten Standort und an den vom Versicherungsnehmer gemieteten Banksafes und dazugehörenden Schlüsseln.» (The clause covers the effective cost of the change, and the claimant must prove the amount (Ziff. 29 lit. a); the invoice of the locksmith or supplier is the document that ordinarily shows that cost. Costs after a simple theft are excluded (Ziff. 20 lit. d, Ausschluss), so the invoice is needed only after a burglary or robbery.)

## Type 2: Simple theft away from the insured location (CSS AVB Ziff. 24.2)

Defining event: Does the file state that household contents were stolen outside the insured location, in a theft that was neither a burglary (the thief forcibly entered the building or a room of it, or broke open a container in it, or unlocked it with the correct keys or codes that the thief had obtained by burglary or robbery) or a robbery (theft under threat or use of violence against the policyholder or a member of the household, or while they could not resist because of unconsciousness, accident or death)?

### Facts

- **theft_away**: Does the file state that household contents were stolen outside the insured location, in a theft that was neither a burglary (the thief forcibly entered the building or a room of it, or broke open a container in it, or unlocked it with the correct keys or codes that the thief had obtained by burglary or robbery) or a robbery (theft under threat or use of violence against the policyholder or a member of the household, or while they could not resist because of unconsciousness, accident or death)? Not this: A theft at the insured location (the other claim type, Ziff. 23.3); items lost, left behind or misplaced. Source: css-avb Ziff. 24.2, S. 8: «Schäden am Hausrat durch Diebstahl, der weder als Einbruchdiebstahl noch als Beraubung gilt und sich ausserhalb des versicherten Standortes ereignet, sind bis zu der in der Police vereinbarten Versicherungssumme mitversichert.»
- **away_cover_missing**: Does the file state that the policy does not include the separate supplementary insurance for simple theft away from home (einfacher Diebstahl auswärts)? Not this: Another supplementary cover being missing; the claimant being unsure whether the cover exists (that is open). Source: css-avb Ziff. 23.3 lit. c, S. 7: «Für den einfachen Diebstahl auswärts ist eine separate Zusatzversicherung abzuschliessen.»
- **traces_changed**: Does the file state that traces of the theft (for example pry marks, a broken lock, window or container) have been removed or altered, or are to be removed or altered, for example by repair or cleaning? Not this: Traces that exist and are left as they were, or were only photographed; the police examining the scene. Source: css-avb Ziff. 8 lit. f, S. 3: «ohne Zustimmung der Polizei die Tatspuren nicht zu entfernen oder zu verändern»
- **bicycle**: Is a bicycle among the stolen or damaged items? Not this: A motor scooter or motorcycle; bicycle parts or accessories alone. Source: css-form Abschnitt 3.9, S. 3: «Fahrrad (bitte Originalkaufbelege beilegen)»
- **vouchers_unobtainable**: Does the file state that the original purchase vouchers for the stolen or damaged items cannot be obtained, or only at considerable cost (not even as a duplicate from the seller)? Not this: The vouchers have not been attached yet or not been looked for; finding them is merely inconvenient. Source: vvg VVG Art. 39 Abs. 2 Ziff. 1: «Der Vertrag kann verfügen: 1. dass der Anspruchsberechtigte bestimmte Belege, deren Beschaffung ihm ohne erhebliche Kosten möglich ist, insbesondere auch ärztliche Bescheinigungen, beizubringen hat;»
- **instruction_due**: Does the file state that measures to reduce the loss are or were to be taken after the theft, and that there was no imminent danger (Gefahr im Verzug) requiring them before the insurer could be asked? Not this: Measures that had to be taken at once because of imminent danger; the notification of the claim itself; steps to find the thief or recover the items. Source: vvg VVG Art. 38a Abs. 1: «Der Anspruchsberechtigte ist verpflichtet, nach Eintritt des befürchteten Ereignisses tunlichst für Minderung des Schadens zu sorgen. Er muss, wenn nicht Gefahr im Verzug liegt, über die zu ergreifenden Massnahmen die Weisung des Versicherungsunternehmens einholen und befolgen.»

### Stops

- Not a simple theft away from the insured location: this claim type does not apply: when theft_away = false. Source: Ziff. 24.2, S. 8: «Schäden am Hausrat durch Diebstahl, der weder als Einbruchdiebstahl noch als Beraubung gilt und sich ausserhalb des versicherten Standortes ereignet, sind bis zu der in der Police vereinbarten Versicherungssumme mitversichert.» (The clause covers damage by this kind of theft outside the insured location; where the file states that no such theft occurred, the steps of this claim type do not apply.)
- No cover: the supplementary insurance for simple theft away was not taken out: when theft_away = true and away_cover_missing = true. Source: Ziff. 23.3 lit. c, S. 7: «Für den einfachen Diebstahl auswärts ist eine separate Zusatzversicherung abzuschliessen.» (Cover for simple theft away requires a separate supplementary insurance; where the policy does not include it, there is no cover for the event and the process for this claim type ends.)

### Steps, in order

- **Check that the reported event is a simple theft away from the insured location** (a_event), reached when always. Source: Ziff. 24.2, S. 8: «Schäden am Hausrat durch Diebstahl, der weder als Einbruchdiebstahl noch als Beraubung gilt und sich ausserhalb des versicherten Standortes ereignet, sind bis zu der in der Police vereinbarten Versicherungssumme mitversichert.»
- **Check that the policy includes the supplementary insurance for simple theft away** (a_cover), reached when theft_away = true. Source: Ziff. 23.3 lit. c, S. 7: «Für den einfachen Diebstahl auswärts ist eine separate Zusatzversicherung abzuschliessen.»
- **Record the claim notification and the policy** (a_notify), reached when theft_away = true and away_cover_missing = false. Source: VVG Art. 38 Abs. 1: «Ist das befürchtete Ereignis eingetreten, so muss der Anspruchsberechtigte, sobald er von diesem Ereignisse und seinem Anspruche aus der Versicherung Kenntnis erlangt, das Versicherungsunternehmen benachrichtigen.»
- **Check that the police were notified and an official investigation requested** (a_police), reached when theft_away = true and away_cover_missing = false. Source: Ziff. 8 lit. f, S. 3: «die Polizeibehörde umgehend zu benachrichtigen, eine amtliche Untersuchung zu beantragen»
- **Check the police's consent to removing or altering the traces** (a_traces), reached when theft_away = true and away_cover_missing = false and traces_changed = true. Source: Ziff. 8 lit. f, S. 3: «ohne Zustimmung der Polizei die Tatspuren nicht zu entfernen oder zu verändern»
- **Check whether the insurer's instruction on loss-reduction measures is to be obtained** (a_instruction_check), reached when theft_away = true and away_cover_missing = false. Source: VVG Art. 38a Abs. 1 Satz 2: «Er muss, wenn nicht Gefahr im Verzug liegt, über die zu ergreifenden Massnahmen die Weisung des Versicherungsunternehmens einholen und befolgen.»
- **Obtain and follow the insurer's instruction on the measures to be taken** (a_instruction), reached when theft_away = true and away_cover_missing = false and instruction_due = true. Source: VVG Art. 38a Abs. 1 Satz 2: «Er muss, wenn nicht Gefahr im Verzug liegt, über die zu ergreifenden Massnahmen die Weisung des Versicherungsunternehmens einholen und befolgen.»
- **Establish the stolen or damaged items and their value** (a_loss), reached when theft_away = true and away_cover_missing = false. Source: Ziff. 29 lit. a, S. 11: «Der Versicherungsnehmer muss die Schadenhöhe beweisen.»

- **Claim notification: the CSS form 'Schadenanzeige Hausrat / Gebäude', completed and signed** (claim_form): step a_notify; needed when always; mandatory; must show: that and when CSS was notified of the theft; the signature under the declaration that all questions were answered truthfully and completely. Source: VVG Art. 38 Abs. 1: «Ist das befürchtete Ereignis eingetreten, so muss der Anspruchsberechtigte, sobald er von diesem Ereignisse und seinem Anspruche aus der Versicherung Kenntnis erlangt, das Versicherungsunternehmen benachrichtigen.» (The passage makes notifying the insurer a duty; the insurer's own claim form, completed and signed, is the document that ordinarily records that and when the insurer was notified.)

Source: Ziff. 24.2, S. 8: «Schäden am Hausrat durch Diebstahl, der weder als Einbruchdiebstahl noch als Beraubung gilt und sich ausserhalb des versicherten Standortes ereignet, sind bis zu der in der Police vereinbarten Versicherungssumme mitversichert.» (The clause covers the damage up to the sum insured agreed in the policy; the policy is the document that shows whether the supplementary cover was agreed and for which sum.)
- **Police report on the theft (Polizeirapport)** (police_report): step a_police; needed when always; only if CSS asks for it (not mandatory); must show: that the theft was reported to the police; that an official investigation was requested. Source: Ziff. 8 lit. f, S. 3: «Ein entsprechender Polizeirapport kann durch die CSS einverlangt werden»

Source: Ziff. 8 lit. f, S. 3: «ohne Zustimmung der Polizei die Tatspuren nicht zu entfernen oder zu verändern» (The passage makes the police's consent the condition for removing or altering traces; once traces have been or are to be removed or altered, the police's written confirmation of that consent is the document that ordinarily shows the condition was met.)

Source: Ziff. 8 lit. c, S. 3: «auf Verlangen, ein Verzeichnis der vom Schaden betroffenen Sachen mit Wertangaben zu erstellen und Originalbelege beizubringen;»

Source: Abschnitt 3.7 Gegenstände, S. 2: «Beschädigte oder gestohlene Sachen (bitte Originalkaufbelege beilegen)»
- **Original purchase voucher for the bicycle** (bicycle_voucher): step a_loss; needed when bicycle = true and vouchers_unobtainable = false; mandatory; must show: the bicycle; where and when it was bought; the price paid. Source: Abschnitt 3.9, S. 3: «Fahrrad (bitte Originalkaufbelege beilegen)»
