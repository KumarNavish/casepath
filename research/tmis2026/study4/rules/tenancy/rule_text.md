# Tenancy rules: which documents to request

These rules come from the Swiss Code of Obligations (SR 220), Title Eight (Leases), consolidated version of 1 October 2026. Article numbers refer to that Code. They apply to a residential tenant's dispute of one of three types: defect of the rented dwelling (mould, damp, heating), termination of the lease by the landlord, rent increase.

How to apply them:

- Each type has deciding questions. Answer each from the claim and its attachments: yes, no, open (the claim raises the matter and leaves it unsettled), or not mentioned.
- A step is reached when its condition holds; a document is requested when its step is reached and its own condition holds. A step or document whose condition depends on an unresolved or open answer waits for that answer.
- If the claim states that the type's defining event did not happen, the process ends and no document is requested.

## Convention of this benchmark (not law)

This is a convention of this benchmark, not a rule of law; it cites no legal text. A fact that the file does not mention is taken as not alleged, and the rules are applied as if it were absent. Silence never ends a process: where a fact's absence would end it, the unmentioned fact is unresolved. The defining event is never presumed: if the file does not mention it, it is unresolved. A fact that the file raises and leaves unsettled is open.

Under each question below, "If not mentioned" says how the convention applies to it.

## 1. Defect of the rented dwelling (mould, damp, heating)

Deciding questions:

1. Defining event (art. 259a para. 1): Does the claim state that the rented dwelling has a defect (for example mould, damp, or heating that fails) or that the tenant is disturbed in using the dwelling as agreed, without the claim itself saying that the tenant caused the defect or must remedy it as minor cleaning or repairs?
   Not this: A defect the claim itself attributes to the tenant, or one it describes as minor cleaning or repairs the tenant must do (art. 259); a landlord's allegation that the tenant caused it, which the claim disputes, does not make this false.
   If not mentioned: unresolved (the defining event is never presumed).
2. Branch (art. 257g para. 1): Does the claim state that the tenant has not yet informed the landlord of the defect?
   Not this: A tenant who has informed the landlord orally or informally, or through the landlord's property manager or caretaker; that tenant has informed the landlord.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
3. Branch (art. 259e): Does the tenant claim compensation from the landlord for damage the defect caused to the tenant (for example damaged furniture or clothing, or extra costs)?
   Not this: A reduction of the rent for the reduced use of the dwelling (art. 259d), which is not compensation for damage.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
4. Branch (art. 259a para. 2): Does the claim state that the tenant intends to pay the rent on deposit with the office designated by the canton instead of to the landlord, or has begun to do so?
   Not this: Withholding or reducing the rent without paying it to a deposit office; the rental security paid at the start of the lease (art. 257e).
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
5. Branch (art. 259h para. 1): Does the claim state that at least one rent payment has already been paid into deposit with the office designated by the canton?
   Not this: A deposit that is announced or planned but not yet made; the rental security paid at the start of the lease.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.

Stop: if question 1 is no, the process ends (art. 259a para. 1: The tenant's rights in this type arise only where a defect not attributable to the tenant arises or the agreed use is disturbed; where the claim states neither, no such right is engaged and the process ends.)

Steps and documents:

- **Establish the defect and the agreed use of the dwelling**, reached when question 1 is yes (art. 256 para. 1: Whether the dwelling is defective is measured against its designated use, so the defect and the agreed use are established once a defect is stated.)
  - Request **Lease contract**. It must show: parties; rented dwelling; agreed use of the dwelling (art. 256 para. 1: The landlord's duty is measured by the designated use; the lease records the rented dwelling and its agreed use.)
  - Request **Record of the defect (dated photographs, inspection or expert report)**. It must show: the defect and where it is; date observed; how the use of the dwelling is affected (art. 259a para. 1: The tenant's rights arise where a defect arises or the agreed use is disturbed; dated photographs or an inspection report ordinarily prove that the defect exists, where it is and since when.)
- **Establish when the landlord learned of the defect**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 2 is no (art. 259b: The tenant's remedies depend on the landlord's knowledge of the defect, so unless the claim states that the landlord has not been informed, when the landlord learned of it is established.)
  - Request **Tenant's notice of the defect to the landlord**. It must show: defect reported; addressee; date sent or received (art. 259d: A reduction runs from the time the landlord became aware of the defect; the tenant's dated notice to the landlord ordinarily proves when that was.)
- **Establish the damage claimed**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 3 is yes (art. 259e: The landlord is liable for damage the defect caused, so a claim for damages requires the damage to be established.)
  - Request **Proof of the damage (invoices, receipts, photographs of damaged items)**. It must show: damaged items or costs; amount; connection to the defect (art. 259e: Liability covers damage the defect caused; invoices, receipts and photographs of damaged items ordinarily prove the damage and its amount.)
- **Check the preconditions for paying rent on deposit**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 4 is yes (art. 259g para. 1: Paying rent on deposit is subject to written steps toward the landlord, so they are checked when a deposit is intended or made.)
  - Request **Tenant's written time limit to the landlord and written announcement of the deposit**. It must show: repair requested; time limit set; announcement that rent will be deposited; date and addressee (art. 259g para. 1: The time limit (and the announcement of the deposit) must be in writing, so the tenant's letter itself is the proof that they were given and when.)
  - Request **Receipt of the deposit office for the deposited rent**, if question 5 is yes. It must show: amount deposited; date of the first deposit; rent period it covers (art. 259h para. 1: The 30-day period runs from the due date of the first deposited rent; the deposit office's receipt ordinarily shows which rent was first deposited and when.)

## 2. Termination of the lease by the landlord

Deciding questions:

1. Defining event (art. 273 para. 1): Has the tenant received a notice from the landlord terminating the lease of the dwelling?
   Not this: A lease of limited duration that ends on its agreed date without notice (art. 266); a termination the landlord has only announced or threatened; a notice given by the tenant.
   If not mentioned: unresolved (the defining event is never presumed).
2. Branch (art. 266n): Does the claim state that the tenant is married or in a registered partnership and that the rented dwelling serves as the family home?
   Not this: A tenant living with a partner who is neither spouse nor registered partner; spouses who do not use the dwelling as their family home.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
3. Branch (art. 257d para. 2): Does the notice or the claim state that the landlord terminated because the tenant did not pay overdue rent or accessory charges within a time limit the landlord had set?
   Not this: Arrears that the claim mentions while the notice is given for another reason (for example the landlord's own use); a dispute about a rent increase.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
4. Branch (art. 271a para. 1 let. a): Does the claim state that the landlord gave notice because the tenant had, in good faith, asserted claims under the lease (for example demanded repair of a defect or contested the rent)?
   Not this: Claims the tenant asserted that the claim does not connect with the notice; a notice the claim attributes to another reason, such as the landlord's own use.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
5. Branch (art. 272 para. 1): Does the tenant seek an extension of the lease because having to leave by the end date would be a hardship for the tenant or the tenant's family?
   Not this: A tenant who only contests the validity of the notice and does not ask for more time; a request for more time that the claim does not base on hardship.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.

Stop: if question 1 is no, the process ends (art. 273 para. 1: The challenge runs from receipt of a notice of termination; where the claim states that no notice was received there is nothing to challenge and the process ends. Not covered: a lease of limited duration ending without notice, where an extension may be requested under art. 273(2)(b); that is outside this type.)

Steps and documents:

- **Check the form of the notice, the notice period and the challenge deadline**, reached when question 1 is yes (art. 266o: A received notice is void if it does not conform to arts. 266l–266n, so its form and service are checked first.)
  - Request **Lease contract**. It must show: parties; rented dwelling; duration (limited or indefinite); agreed notice periods and termination dates (art. 266a para. 1: Whether the notice respects the notice period and termination date depends on what the parties agreed; the lease records the duration and any agreed periods and dates.)
  - Request **Landlord's notice of termination or written threat of termination**. It must show: sender and addressee; date; termination date; whether the form approved by the canton is used (art. 266l para. 2: The landlord's notice must be given on a form approved by the canton (and in writing, art. 266l(1)); only the notice itself shows whether it was.)
  - Request **Proof of the date the notice was received**. It must show: date of receipt; how it was delivered (registered-mail tracking, envelope or acknowledgement) (art. 273 para. 1: The challenge must be brought within 30 days of receiving the notice; proof of the date of receipt is needed to check that period.)
- **Check separate service on the spouse or registered partner**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 2 is yes (art. 266n: “Notice of termination given by the landlord and any notification of a time limit for payment accompanied by a warning of termination in the event of non-payment (Art. 257d) must be served separately on the tenant and on his spouse or registered partner.”)
  - Request **Copy of the notice served separately on the spouse or registered partner**. It must show: addressed to the spouse or registered partner; served separately from the tenant's copy; date; where the notice followed a payment time limit (art. 257d): the separately served copy of that time limit (art. 266n: Separate service on the spouse or registered partner is required (a notice that does not conform is void, art. 266o); the copy addressed to the spouse ordinarily proves whether it was made.)
- **Check the payment time limit that preceded the notice**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 3 is yes (art. 257d para. 1: A termination for arrears presupposes a time limit for payment set beforehand, so that time limit is checked.)
  - Request **Landlord's written payment time limit with warning of termination**. It must show: amount in arrears; time limit set; warning that the lease will be terminated; date of receipt (art. 257d para. 1: A termination for arrears requires a prior time limit of at least 30 days for a dwelling with a warning of termination; the German text requires it in writing ('schriftlich', missing from the English), so the landlord's letter itself shows the time limit and the warning.)
- **Check whether the notice can be challenged as contrary to good faith**, reached when question 1 is yes (art. 271 para. 1: Any received notice can be challenged on this ground, so the step is reached whenever a notice was received; the documents it needs depend on the grounds the claim states.)
  - Request **Tenant's earlier correspondence asserting claims under the lease**, if question 4 is yes. It must show: claim asserted (for example a repair demand or a rent challenge); date; addressee (art. 271a para. 1 let. a: The notice may be challenged where it was given because the tenant asserted claims; the tenant's dated letters show which claims were asserted and when, relative to the notice.)
- **Assess an extension of the lease**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 5 is yes and question 3 is no (art. 272a para. 1 let. a: An extension is assessed where the tenant seeks it (art. 272(1)), except where notice was given for arrears under art. 257d, for which art. 272a(1)(a) excludes it.)
  - Request **Evidence of the tenant's personal, family and financial circumstances**. It must show: household members; financial circumstances; personal or health circumstances (art. 272 para. 2 let. c: In weighing an extension the authority has particular regard to the parties' personal, family and financial circumstances; documents of the tenant's circumstances ordinarily prove them.)

## 3. Rent increase

Deciding questions:

1. Defining event (art. 270b para. 1): Does the claim state that the landlord has communicated to the tenant an increase of the rent of the dwelling (whether or not on an official form)?
   Not this: A landlord's plan to raise the rent that has not yet been communicated; the initial rent of a new lease (art. 270); new accessory charges or reduced services without a rent increase (art. 269d para. 3).
   If not mentioned: unresolved (the defining event is never presumed).
2. Branch (art. 270d): Does the claim state that the increase is a step fixed in advance in the lease, by which the rent rises periodically by fixed amounts (graduated rent)?
   Not this: An increase under an index clause; an increase the lease does not fix in advance.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
3. Branch (art. 270c): Does the claim state that the increase applies a clause of the lease linking the rent to an index (such as the Swiss consumer price index)?
   Not this: An increase the landlord justifies by general inflation or by cost increases without an index clause in the lease.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
4. Always stated (art. 269a let. b): Does the increase notice give, as its reason, higher costs for the landlord (for example higher mortgage interest or operating costs) or additional services or works by the landlord (for example a renovation)?
   Not this: An increase justified only by rents customary in the locality (art. 269a let. a) or by inflation on the risk capital (art. 269a let. e).
   If not mentioned: unresolved (this fact has no ordinary default).
5. Branch (art. 269d para. 2 let. c): Does the claim state that, together with the rent increase, the landlord gave notice of termination or threatened to terminate the lease?
   Not this: A notice of termination given separately and at another time, not together with the increase.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.

Stop: if question 1 is no, the process ends (art. 270b para. 1: The tenant's challenge attaches to a communicated increase; where the claim states that no increase was communicated there is nothing to challenge or check and the process ends.)

Steps and documents:

- **Check the form, reasons and timing of the increase notice**, reached when question 1 is yes (art. 269d para. 1: A communicated increase must be notified with reasons, in time and on the approved form, so these are checked first.)
  - Request **Lease contract**. It must show: parties; rented dwelling; termination dates; any graduated-rent or index clause (art. 269d para. 1: The increase can take effect only from the next termination date; the lease records the termination dates (and any graduated-rent or index clause, arts. 269b–269c).)
  - Request **Landlord's rent increase notice**. It must show: old and new rent; date from which the increase applies; reasons given; whether the form approved by the canton is used (art. 269d para. 2 let. a–b: An increase is void without the prescribed form or without reasons; only the notice itself shows the form used and the reasons given.)
  - Request **Proof of the date the notice was received**. It must show: date of receipt; how it was delivered (registered-mail tracking, envelope or acknowledgement) (art. 269d para. 1: The notice must reach the tenant at least ten days before the notice period begins; proof of the date of receipt shows whether it did (and fixes the start of the 30-day challenge period, art. 270b(1)).)
- **Check the graduated-rent agreement**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 2 is yes (art. 269c: “An agreement to increase the rent periodically by fixed amounts is valid only where:
a. the lease is contracted for at least three years;
b. the rent is increased no more than once a year; and
c. the amount by which it is increased is fixed in francs.”)
- **Check the index clause**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 2 is no and question 3 is yes (art. 269b: “An agreement to link rent to an index is valid only where the lease is contracted for at least five years and the benchmark is the Swiss consumer prices index.”)
- **Check whether the increase is justified by its stated reasons**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 2 is no and question 3 is no (art. 270b para. 1: An increase that is neither a graduated step (art. 270d) nor index-linked (art. 270c) can be challenged as unfair under arts. 269 and 269a, so its reasons are tested.)
  - Request **Landlord's breakdown of the cost increases or additional services relied on**, if question 4 is yes. It must show: costs or works relied on; amounts; share passed on to the rent (art. 269a let. b: A rent justified by cost increases or additional services is in general not unfair; the landlord's breakdown of those costs or works ordinarily shows whether the increase is so justified.)
- **Check whether the increase was accompanied by a notice or threat of termination**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 5 is yes (art. 269d para. 2 let. c: “c. notice of the increase is accompanied by notice to terminate or a threat of termination.”)
  - Request **Landlord's notice of termination or written threat of termination**. It must show: sender and addressee; date; termination or threat stated; connection to the rent increase (art. 269d para. 2 let. c: The increase is void if accompanied by a notice or a threat of termination; the notice or the letter containing the threat shows whether it accompanied the increase.)
