# Tenancy rules: which documents to request

These rules come from the Swiss Code of Obligations (SR 220), Title Eight (Leases), consolidated version of 1 October 2026. Article numbers refer to that Code. They apply to a residential tenant's dispute of one of three types: defect of the rented dwelling or disturbance of its use (for example mould, damp, heating), termination of the lease by the landlord, rent increase.

How to apply them:

- Each type has deciding questions. Answer each from the claim and its attachments: yes, no, open (the claim raises the matter and leaves it unsettled), or not mentioned.
- A step is reached when its condition holds; a document is requested when its step is reached and its own condition holds. A step or document whose condition depends on an unresolved or open answer waits for that answer.
- If the claim states that the type's defining event did not happen, the process ends and no document is requested.

## How to read steps and stops

Steps are taken in the order given. A document belongs to a step and is decided when that step is reached. A stop ends the process at its step: no later step is reached, and no document of a later step is requested or left waiting. Documents of steps reached before the stop keep the state they had; a document requested there remains requested.

## Convention of this benchmark (not law)

This is a convention of this benchmark, not a rule of law; it cites no legal text. A fact that the file does not mention is taken as not alleged, and the rules are applied as if it were absent. Silence never ends a process: where a fact's absence would end it, the unmentioned fact is unresolved. The defining event is never presumed: if the file does not mention it, it is unresolved. A fact that the file raises and leaves unsettled is open.

Under each question below, "If not mentioned" says how the convention applies to it.

## Which type applies (routing)

Routing question: Which tenancy dispute does the claim concern: a defect of the dwelling or a disturbance of its agreed use that the tenant did not cause (for example mould, damp, or heating that fails), a termination of the lease by the landlord, or a rent increase? A rent increase that the landlord accompanied with a notice of termination is a rent increase dispute: the rent-increase rules examine that notice (art. 269d para. 2 let. c).

Values: defect_mold_heating, lease_termination_dispute, rent_increase_dispute (in the order of the question's three disputes). The question carries its own limits; it has no separate not_this line.

## 1. Defect of the rented dwelling or disturbance of its use (for example mould, damp, heating)

Deciding questions:

1. Defining event (art. 259a para. 1): Does the claim state that the rented dwelling has a defect (for example mould, damp, or heating that fails) or that the tenant is disturbed in using the dwelling as agreed, without the claim itself saying that the tenant alone caused the defect or must remedy it at his own expense?
   Not this: A defect the claim itself attributes wholly to the tenant, or says he must remedy at his own expense, is no. A cause the claim calls shared or disputed is open, not no; a landlord's allegation that the tenant caused it, which the claim disputes, does not make this no.
   If not mentioned: unresolved (the defining event is never presumed).
2. Branch (art. 257g para. 2): Does the claim state that the landlord does not yet know of the defect (the tenant has not reported it and the landlord has not learned of it in another way)?
   Not this: A landlord who learned of the defect from the caretaker, the property manager or an inspection, although the tenant never wrote: that landlord knows, so the answer is no.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
3. Branch (art. 257g para. 1): Does the claim state that the landlord learned of the defect only orally, so that no written notice, written acknowledgement or inspection record of it exists?
   Not this: A tenant who told the landlord by telephone and later confirmed it by letter, e-mail or text message: a written trace exists, so the answer is no.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
4. Branch (art. 259e): Does the tenant claim compensation from the landlord for damage the defect caused to the tenant (for example damaged furniture or clothing, or extra costs)?
   Not this: A reduction of the rent for the reduced use of the dwelling (art. 259d), which is not compensation for damage.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
5. Branch (art. 259a para. 2): Does the claim state that the tenant intends to pay the rent on deposit with the office designated by the canton instead of to the landlord, or has begun to do so?
   Not this: Withholding or reducing the rent without paying it to a deposit office; the rental security paid at the start of the lease.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
6. Branch (art. 259h para. 1): Does the claim state that at least one rent payment has already been paid into deposit with the office designated by the canton?
   Not this: A deposit that is announced or planned but not yet made; the rental security paid at the start of the lease.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.

Stop: if question 1 is no, the process ends (art. 259a para. 1: The tenant's rights in this type arise only where a defect not attributable to the tenant arises or the agreed use is disturbed; where the claim states neither, or attributes the defect wholly to the tenant, no such right is engaged and the process ends. A shared or disputed cause is open and does not stop.)

Steps and documents:

- **Establish the defect and the agreed use of the dwelling**, reached when question 1 is yes (art. 256 para. 1: Whether the dwelling is defective is measured against its designated use, so the defect and the agreed use are established once a defect is stated.)
  - Request **Lease contract**. It must show: parties; rented dwelling; agreed use of the dwelling (art. 256 para. 1: The landlord's duty is measured by the designated use; the lease records the rented dwelling and its agreed use.)
  - Request **Record of the defect (dated photographs, inspection or expert report)**. It must show: the defect and where it is; date observed; how the use of the dwelling is affected (art. 259a para. 1: The tenant's rights arise where a defect arises or the agreed use is disturbed; dated photographs or an inspection report ordinarily prove that the defect exists, where it is and since when.)
- **Establish how and when the landlord learned of the defect**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 2 is no (art. 259b: The tenant's remedies under art. 259b depend on the landlord's knowledge, so when the landlord learned of the defect is established.)
  - Request **Proof of when the landlord learned of the defect (tenant's dated notice, landlord's or manager's acknowledgement, or inspection record)**, if question 3 is no. It must show: how and when the landlord learned of the defect; the defect reported; who was informed (art. 259b, with art. 259d: The remedies depend on the landlord being aware of the defect, and a reduction runs from the time he became aware (art. 259d); a dated written notice, a written acknowledgement or an inspection record ordinarily proves when that was. Where the claim states that the landlord was told only orally, no such record exists and none is requested.)
- **Establish the damage claimed**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 4 is yes (art. 259e: The landlord is liable for damage the defect caused, so a claim for damages requires the damage to be established.)
  - Request **Proof of the damage (invoices, receipts, photographs of damaged items)**. It must show: damaged items or costs; amount; connection to the defect (art. 259e: Liability covers damage the defect caused; invoices, receipts and photographs of damaged items ordinarily prove the damage and its amount.)
- **Check the preconditions and the time limit of the rent deposit**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 5 is yes (art. 259g para. 1: Paying rent on deposit is subject to written steps toward the landlord, so they are checked when a deposit is intended or made.)
  - Request **Tenant's written time limit to the landlord and written announcement of the deposit**. It must show: repair requested; time limit set; announcement that rent will be deposited; date and addressee (art. 259g para. 1: The time limit (and the announcement of the deposit) must be in writing, so the tenant's letter itself is the proof that they were given and when.)
  - Request **Receipt of the deposit office for the deposited rent and the tenant's application to the conciliation authority**, if question 6 is yes. It must show: amount deposited; due date of the first deposited rent; the date on which the claims were brought before the conciliation authority, or a sentence that they have not yet been brought (art. 259h para. 1: The deposited rent passes to the landlord unless claims are brought before the conciliation authority within 30 days of the due date of the first deposited rent; the deposit office's receipt shows that due date and the application shows whether and when the claims were brought.)

## 2. Termination of the lease by the landlord

Deciding questions:

1. Defining event (art. 273 para. 1): Has the landlord given the tenant notice terminating the lease of the dwelling, and has it reached the tenant?
   Not this: No: only a file stating that no notice of termination has been given at all (also a lease of limited duration that simply ends on its agreed date, a termination only announced or threatened, a notice given by the tenant). A notice the landlord says was sent and the tenant says never reached him is open, not no.
   If not mentioned: unresolved (the defining event is never presumed).
2. Branch (art. 266m para. 1): Does the claim state that the tenant is married or in a registered partnership and that the rented dwelling serves as the family home?
   Not this: A tenant living with a partner who is neither spouse nor registered partner; spouses who do not use the dwelling as their family home.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
3. Branch (art. 257d para. 2): Does the notice or the claim state that the landlord terminated because the tenant did not pay overdue rent or accessory charges within a time limit the landlord had set?
   Not this: Arrears that the claim mentions while the notice is given for another reason (for example the landlord's own use); a dispute about a rent increase.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
4. Branch (art. 271a para. 1 let. a): Does the claim state that the landlord gave notice because the tenant had, in good faith, asserted claims under the lease (for example demanded repair of a defect or contested the rent)? The tenant's own account of the landlord's reason counts as stating it, also where the tenant gives it as a conviction or belief; it is open only where the claim itself says the reason is unknown, uncertain or one of several possibilities.
   Not this: Claims the tenant asserted that the claim does not connect with the notice; a notice the claim attributes to another reason, such as the landlord's own use.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
5. Branch (art. 272 para. 1): Does the tenant seek an extension of the lease because having to leave by the end date would be a hardship for the tenant or the tenant's family?
   Not this: A tenant who only contests the validity of the notice and does not ask for more time; a request for more time that the claim does not base on hardship.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.

Stop: if question 1 is no, the process ends (art. 273 para. 1: The challenge runs from receipt of a notice of termination; where the claim states that the landlord gave no notice of termination there is nothing to challenge and the process ends. A notice whose receipt is disputed is open and does not stop. Not covered: a lease of limited duration ending without notice, where an extension may be requested under art. 273 para. 2 let. b; that is outside this type.)

Steps and documents:

- **Check the form of the notice, the notice period and the challenge deadline**, reached when question 1 is yes (art. 266o: A received notice is void if it does not conform to arts. 266l–266n, so its form and service are checked first.)
  - Request **Lease contract**. It must show: parties; rented dwelling; duration (limited or indefinite); agreed notice periods and termination dates (art. 266a para. 1: Whether the notice respects the notice period and termination date depends on what the parties agreed; the lease records the duration and any agreed periods and dates.)
  - Request **Landlord's notice of termination**. It must show: sender and addressee; date; termination date; given in writing; a statement of the form used: the printed title of the form approved by the canton, or a sentence on the notice that it is or is not given on that form (art. 266l para. 2, with art. 266l para. 1: The landlord's notice must be given on a form approved by the canton (and in writing, art. 266l para. 1); only the notice itself shows whether it was.)
  - Request **Proof of the date the notice was received**. It must show: date of receipt; how it was delivered (registered-mail tracking, envelope or acknowledgement) (art. 273 para. 1: The challenge must be brought within 30 days of receiving the notice; proof of the date of receipt is needed to check that period.)
- **Check separate service on the spouse or registered partner**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 2 is yes (art. 266n, with art. 266m para. 1: The passage states the duty of separate service; the family-home condition under which the step is reached comes from the marginal heading 'Family residence' and art. 266m para. 1 (see family_residence).)
  - Request **Proof of separate service on the spouse or registered partner (the spouse's own copy with envelope or tracking), or the spouse's statement that no separate copy was received**. It must show: addressed to the spouse or registered partner; served separately from the tenant's copy; date; Only if the file states that the landlord terminated because rent or accessory charges were not paid within a payment time limit (art. 257d): the copy of that payment time limit served separately on the spouse or registered partner. Otherwise this item does not apply. (art. 266n: Separate service on the spouse or registered partner is required (a notice that does not conform is void, art. 266o); the spouse's own copy ordinarily proves it was made, and the spouse's statement records that it was not.)
- **Check the payment time limit that preceded the notice and the payments made within it**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 3 is yes (art. 257d para. 1: A termination for arrears presupposes a time limit for payment set beforehand (in writing: 'schriftlich' in the German, missing from the English), so that time limit is checked.)
  - Request **Landlord's written payment time limit with warning of termination, and the tenant's record of payments made within it**. It must show: amount in arrears; time limit set and date it was received; warning that the lease will be terminated; payments made within the time limit (bank or postal records) (art. 257d para. 2, with art. 257d para. 1: Termination is open to the landlord only after non-payment within the time limit; the landlord's written time limit shows the limit and the warning, and the tenant's payment records show whether payment was made within it.)
- **Check whether the notice can be challenged as contrary to good faith**, reached when question 1 is yes (art. 271 para. 1: Any received notice can be challenged on this ground, so the step is reached whenever a notice was received; the documents it needs depend on the grounds the claim states.)
  - Request **Correspondence on the tenant's claims and the landlord's reasons for the notice**, if question 4 is yes. It must show: claims the tenant asserted (for example a repair demand or a rent challenge), with dates; the reasons the landlord gave for the notice (on the notice, or given on request); Only if the file states that the tenant's claims were brought before a conciliation authority or a court: the conciliation or court documents on those claims. Otherwise this item does not apply. (art. 271a para. 1 let. a, with art. 271 para. 2: The notice may be challenged where it was given because the tenant asserted claims; the tenant's dated letters show which claims were asserted and when, and the landlord's stated reasons (which must be given on request, art. 271 para. 2) show the ground he relies on.)
- **Assess an extension of the lease**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 3 is no (art. 272a para. 1 let. a, with art. 273 para. 5: An extension can be at issue after any received notice, whether the tenant requests it or the authority examines it ex officio after rejecting a challenge (art. 273 para. 5); this passage excludes it where notice was given for arrears.)
  - Request **Evidence of the tenant's personal, family and financial circumstances**, if question 5 is yes. It must show: household members; financial circumstances; personal or health circumstances (art. 272 para. 2 let. c: In weighing an extension the authority has particular regard to the parties' personal, family and financial circumstances; documents of the tenant's circumstances ordinarily prove them. They are requested where the tenant seeks an extension. Not requesting them when the authority would examine an extension only ex officio (art. 273 para. 5) is this rule set's choice; that paragraph does not say so.)

## 3. Rent increase

Deciding questions:

1. Defining event (art. 269d para. 2 let. a): Does the claim state that the landlord has announced, demanded or charged a higher rent for the dwelling, by a notice, a letter, an invoice or otherwise (whether or not on an official form)?
   Not this: A landlord's plan to raise the rent that has not been announced, demanded or charged; the initial rent agreed for a new lease; new accessory charges or reduced services without a higher rent.
   If not mentioned: unresolved (the defining event is never presumed).
2. Branch (art. 270d): Does the claim state that the increase is a step fixed in advance in the lease, by which the rent rises periodically by fixed amounts (graduated rent)?
   Not this: An increase under an index clause; an increase the lease does not fix in advance.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
3. Branch (art. 270c): Does the claim state that the increase applies a clause of the lease linking the rent to an index (such as the Swiss consumer price index)?
   Not this: An increase the landlord justifies by general inflation or by cost increases without an index clause in the lease.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.
4. Always stated (art. 269a let. b): Does the increase notice give, as its reason, higher costs for the landlord (for example higher mortgage interest or operating costs) or additional services or works by the landlord (for example a renovation)?
   Not this: An increase justified only by rents customary in the locality (art. 269a let. a) or by inflation on the risk capital (art. 269a let. e).
   If not mentioned: unresolved (this fact has no ordinary default).
5. Branch (art. 269d para. 2 let. c): Does the claim state that, together with the rent increase, the landlord gave notice of termination?
   Not this: A threat of termination without a notice of termination (it shows on the increase notice or its covering letter, which is requested anyway); a notice of termination given separately at another time.
   If not mentioned: taken as no.

Stop: if question 1 is no, the process ends (art. 270b para. 1: The tenant's challenge and the voidness rules attach to an increase; where the claim states that the landlord has neither announced nor demanded nor charged a higher rent there is nothing to challenge or check and the process ends.)

Steps and documents:

- **Check the form, reasons and timing of the increase**, reached when question 1 is yes (art. 269d para. 1, with art. 269d para. 5: An increase must be notified with reasons, in time and on the approved form, so these are checked first; for a graduated-rent step, written form suffices instead of the form (art. 269d para. 5).)
  - Request **Lease contract**. It must show: parties; rented dwelling; termination dates; Only if the file states that the increase is a graduated-rent step or applies an index clause: the lease clause that fixes the graduated steps or links the rent to an index. Otherwise this item does not apply. (art. 269d para. 1: The increase can take effect only from the next termination date; the lease records the termination dates (and any graduated-rent or index clause, arts. 269b–269c).)
  - Request **Landlord's rent increase notice**. It must show: old and new rent; date from which the increase applies; reasons given; a statement of the form used: the printed title of the form approved by the canton, or a sentence on the notice that it is or is not given on that form; for an increase the file states is a graduated-rent step, a written notice suffices instead (art. 269d para. 5); Only if the file states that the landlord gave notice of termination together with the rent increase: the notice or threat of termination in the increase notice or its covering letter. Otherwise this item does not apply.; Only if the file states that the increase applies an index clause: the old and the new index value. Otherwise this item does not apply. (art. 269d para. 2 let. a–b, with art. 269d para. 5: An increase is void without the prescribed form or without reasons; only the notice itself shows the form used and the reasons given, and where no notice exists its absence is the point to establish.)
  - Request **Proof of the date the notice was received**. It must show: date of receipt; how it was delivered (registered-mail tracking, envelope or acknowledgement) (art. 270b para. 1, with art. 269d para. 1: The challenge runs 30 days from receiving the notice (German: from its being communicated); proof of the date of receipt is needed to check that period, and it also shows when the notice was given relative to the ten-day rule of art. 269d para. 1.)
- **Check the graduated-rent agreement**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 2 is yes (art. 269c: “An agreement to increase the rent periodically by fixed amounts is valid only where:
a. the lease is contracted for at least three years;
b. the rent is increased no more than once a year; and
c. the amount by which it is increased is fixed in francs.”)
- **Check the index clause**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 2 is no and question 3 is yes (art. 269b: “An agreement to link rent to an index is valid only where the lease is contracted for at least five years and the benchmark is the Swiss consumer prices index.”)
- **Check whether the increase is justified by its stated reasons**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 2 is no and question 3 is no (art. 270b para. 1: An increase that is neither a graduated step (art. 270d) nor index-linked (art. 270c) can be challenged as unfair under arts. 269 and 269a, so its reasons are tested.)
  - Request **Landlord's breakdown of the cost increases or additional services, with the supporting invoices, interest notices or cost statements**, if question 4 is yes. It must show: costs or works relied on; amounts; share passed on to the rent; supporting invoices or statements (art. 269a let. b: A rent justified by cost increases or additional services is in general not unfair; the underlying invoices, interest notices or cost statements, with the landlord's breakdown, ordinarily show whether the increase is so justified.)
- **Check the notice of termination given with the increase**, reached when question 1 is yes and question 5 is yes (art. 269d para. 2 let. c: “c. notice of the increase is accompanied by notice to terminate or a threat of termination.”)
  - Request **Landlord's notice of termination**. It must show: sender and addressee; date; termination date; connection to the rent increase (art. 269d para. 2 let. c: The increase is void if accompanied by a notice of termination; the notice shows whether it accompanied the increase.)
