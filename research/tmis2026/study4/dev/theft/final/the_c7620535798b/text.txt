Dear Mr Turner,

I've put off writing this for weeks, because every time I sit down to it I end up reliving the evening. My friend Julia Foster finally sat down with me tonight so I could get it done.

On Wednesday 1 July 2026, at about half past ten at night, I was walking from the cinema in Bern towards the station. In the underpass near the university a man stepped out in front of me with a knife. He held it close to my stomach and told me to hand over my bag and my watch, or he'd use it. I was terrified and did exactly what he said. He grabbed the bag off my shoulder, pulled the watch from my wrist himself, and ran up the steps towards the street. The whole thing can't have taken more than half a minute.

In the bag were my phone, my laptop, which I'd brought from work, my wallet and my reading glasses. The watch was a present for my fortieth birthday. Altogether I estimate it at CHF 6,100.

I still don't walk through underpasses, and I take a taxi home from Burgdorf station at night now, which I never used to do. I'm not asking for that to be covered, I just want you to understand why this has taken me so long.

I'd be grateful if you could tell me how to go on from here.

Kind regards,
Alice Morgan
Birkenweg 59, Burgdorf
