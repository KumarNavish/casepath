Clause 2 - Duration
The employment begins on 18 June 2025. It is concluded for a fixed term and ends without notice on 17 June 2027.

Clause 3 - Salary

Clause 7 - Early termination
Before the end of the fixed term, either party may terminate this contract early with a notice period of three months to the end of a calendar month. This applies from the first day of employment.

Amendments to this contract must be made in writing.
