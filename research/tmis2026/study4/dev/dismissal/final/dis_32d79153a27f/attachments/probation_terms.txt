Clause 4 - Probation period

4.1 The probation period is three months.

4.2 During the probation period the parties will hold at least one review meeting to discuss the employee's performance and integration into the team.

4.3 At the end of the probation period the employee receives a short written assessment from the line manager.

4.4 Holidays should, where possible, not be taken during the probation period.
