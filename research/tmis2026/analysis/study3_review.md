## R3. Fact-level accuracy of the product's values (tenancy)

This compares, fact by fact, the value the product used with the designed state of the fact, and counts how often all readings agreed on a wrong value.

- Product value = what the evaluator used: an established fact with verdict 'accepted' (admitted), a presumption, or nothing (unresolved: no reading gave a value; conflicting: readings gave different values; not accepted; absent: the case-run was routed to another workflow or failed). Truth = the designed true value (scenario, defining event, open fact's recorded value). For a designed open fact the right outcome is unresolved.
- Readings: the reading responses of each case-run in the response cache (calls whose answer is a 'conditions' object), combined as team._reading does: a response that answers fewer facts than the workflow has is the requote of the reading before it. Agreement uses the raw verdicts (true / false / open / not_stated) before the quotation check.

## R5. Theft inference: sensitivity to the unit

- registered: as stored in study3_results_fresh2_confirm.json (nine changed-fact clusters; percentile cluster-bootstrap interval, 10,000 resamples)
- stories: exact two-sided sign-flip test over the eight stories (story value = mean over its nine changed-fact pairs and three runs); 2^8 = 256 patterns, smallest p 0.0078
- twoway: 10,000 draws; stories (8) and changed facts (9) resampled independently with replacement; statistic = mean of the cell differences (cell = story x fact, mean over three runs) over the drawn 8 x 9 grid; percentile 2.5-97.5

**Why H4's Self-consistency interval excludes zero while p = 0.0625.** The nine changed-fact differences (CasePath minus Self-consistency) are PR_repair_over_500 -1.750, PR_declined -1.667, PR_nachfrist_set -0.875, PR_goods_recovered -0.750, PR_bicycle_claimed -0.625, PR_scheduled_valuable_1000 -0.583, PR_sim_card_stolen -0.500, PR_expert_procedure_requested +0.125, PR_third_party_causer +1.000; mean -0.625. 7 are negative and 2 positive (PR_expert_procedure_requested, PR_third_party_causer). The percentile bootstrap resamples these nine values with replacement and asks how the mean varies if the nine were a sample from a population like themselves: because almost all of them are negative, few resamples have a mean at or above zero, so the 95% interval [-1.125, -0.079] excludes zero. The exact test asks a different question: under the null each difference is as likely to have the opposite sign, and p is the share of the 2^9 = 512 sign patterns whose absolute sum is at least the observed 5.625. 32 patterns reach it (p = 32/512 = 0.0625): the differences are unequal in size, so flipping the signs of a few small ones still leaves sums as large as the observed one, while the magnitude-blind percentile interval with nine clusters is known to be too narrow. The inverted sign-flip interval, [-1.250, +0.083], includes zero, in agreement with the exact test.
