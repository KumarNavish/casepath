## Method

- Per-case value of a design = mean over its stored runs on the case (three runs; one for the frontier checklist-only product and its lint variants), scored by study3.Study (study2.ten_row; theft: study2.theft_rows, wrong + missed on the 72 changed-fact pairs). Means reproduce study3_feedback.json F1 (checked).
- Claim types: study3_feedback.claim_type (S 40, O 56); O split by the spec's subsets: open defining event (open_event, 9), denied defining event (denied_event, 24), open branch fact only (open_branch_fact, 23).
- Normal approximation: n = (z_{1-alpha/2} + z_power)^2 SD^2 / delta^2 (superiority), (z_{1-alpha} + z_power)^2 SD^2 / (margin - delta)^2 (non-inferiority), SD = sample SD of Study 3's per-case differences.
- Simulation: per target, 2,000 simulated studies resample the observed per-case differences with replacement (shifted by a constant to the target mean for the half-difference and non-inferiority targets), with common random numbers across n; sign-flip test exact up to 12 cases, else 1,000 random sign patterns, p = (1 + count) / 1,001; superiority counts rejections in the target's direction; n found by doubling and bisection up to 2,000.
- Cluster structure: one-way ANOVA by family (tenancy) and by changed fact and by story (theft), and a two-way changed fact x story ANOVA without replication (variance components truncated at zero).
- Lint variants: recovered: lint_followup.theft_variant per-pair rows (captured), reproduce lint_followup.json by fact and Study 3's stored rows (tested).

## Limits

- Zero-inflated differences: tenancy strict_unsafe S CasePath vs Rules in context: 55% of cases tie (52% both zero); tenancy strict_unsafe S CasePath vs Self-consistency: 62% of cases tie (60% both zero); tenancy strict_unsafe all CasePath vs Model executes rules: 57% of cases tie (52% both zero); tenancy strict_unsafe S CasePath vs Model executes rules: 72% of cases tie (68% both zero); tenancy strict_unsafe all CasePath vs Rules in context, frontier: 52% of cases tie (47% both zero); tenancy strict_unsafe S CasePath vs Rules in context, frontier: 75% of cases tie (72% both zero); tenancy all_dev S CasePath vs Rules in context, frontier: 52% of cases tie (45% both zero); tenancy strict_unsafe all CasePath, checklist only, frontier (one run) vs Rules in context, frontier: 52% of cases tie (46% both zero); tenancy strict_unsafe S CasePath, checklist only, frontier (one run) vs Rules in context, frontier: 78% of cases tie (78% both zero); tenancy all_dev all CasePath, checklist only, frontier (one run) vs Rules in context, frontier: 53% of cases tie (44% both zero); tenancy all_dev S CasePath, checklist only, frontier (one run) vs Rules in context, frontier: 75% of cases tie (72% both zero); theft errors changed-fact pairs Frontier checklist only, lint a (in-sample flags, all flagged conditions open, one run) vs Rules in context, original rules, frontier: 54% of cases tie (32% both zero); theft errors changed-fact pairs Frontier checklist only, lint b (in-sample flags, case-type conditions true, one run) vs Rules in context, original rules, frontier: 54% of cases tie (32% both zero). The sign-flip test gets its power only from non-tied cases; shifting such differences to a half-size target (simulation) removes the ties and is optimistic.
- Floor effects: on S claims most designs make few strict unsafe errors, so the S mean differences are small and rest on few non-zero cases; their SDs and means are estimated from 40 claims in 24 families.
- Study 3's cases are not independent: per-case SDs are marginal over crossed clusters (families; changed facts x stories). A replication that samples different families or changed facts will see a different mean difference; where the between-cluster share is large the observed mean is a weak guide to the replication's.
- Observed differences used as the true effect are selected (post hoc) and noisy; the 'half' target is the more prudent planning value.
- The frontier checklist-only product has one run: its per-case value includes the whole run-to-run variance, so its SD is larger than a three-run replication would have (see the one-run section), and its mean is one draw.
- Small n: with many tied cases the two-sided sign-flip test's rejection rate under a recentred null reaches 0.10-0.20 at n = 10 and 0.07-0.12 at n = 20 for some tenancy contrasts (type I error section); sample sizes below about 40 cases rest on a few non-tied Study 3 cases and should not be used as planned sizes. The non-inferiority simulations that give n of 5-17 for the frontier checklist-only product on S claims are of this kind (75% of its S cases tie).
- Lint variants: (a) and (b) give identical per-pair values, as do (c_a) and (c_b), as in lint_followup.json.
- The theft measure exists only on changed-fact pairs, each built from a shared off text of a story; a replication with each case its own story removes the story sharing that Study 3 had.
