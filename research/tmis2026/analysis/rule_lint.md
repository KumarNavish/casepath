# Theft rule base: conditions that do not distinguish ordinary cases

Generated by `analysis/rule_lint.py` from case texts, stored readings and the rule base; no model calls. A branch condition should mark what distinguishes a case from the ordinary case of its type. The conditions below are read as true in most of a set of ordinary theft case documents. For each, decide whether it belongs to every theft case (its documents would then be due in every case) or whether it is broader than its source and needs narrowing.

**Counts** (per model; a run is one product run of a document, with three independent readings):

- *ordinary runs*: runs of ordinary case documents (identical reading responses counted once);
- *admitted true*: runs in which every reading answered true with a quotation found in the document;
- *some reading true*: runs in which at least one reading answered true;
- *product admitted*: runs in which the product recorded the condition as established true;
- *unstable*: variant documents of the same cases (which differ from the ordinary document in one sentence that the condition was not read from) in which the condition's value differs from its value in the ordinary document, out of all such observations.

A condition is listed when it is admitted true in at least half of the ordinary runs of a model, or some reading answers true in at least three quarters of them.
