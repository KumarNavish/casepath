\paragraph{Reproducing without model calls}
One command, \texttt{reproduce.sh}, re-checks the stored results from a clone without model calls: data manifests, the parity hash, the claims of Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}, Study~3's registered analysis, the analyses added after review and every number macro, byte for byte. Several analyses added after review re-read the product's response cache (the readings and cost split of \texttt{study3\_review.py}; \texttt{study3\_feedback.py}, \texttt{decomposition.py} and \texttt{lint\_followup.py}). With the cache archive in place they are re-run and compared byte for byte; without it, the command regenerates their macros from the stored result files, runs the tests that need no cache, and reports the re-runs as skipped. It does not re-check the model runs, whose stored outputs are its inputs. A re-run with model calls needs the reader's own model endpoint, and its answers may differ.

\paragraph{Generative AI}
The two evaluated models are the objects of study. An AI agent session wrote the test texts, and sessions of another model of the same family checked them blind. AI editor sessions reviewed the theft rules and the lint's flags; AI sessions wrote the clean-room references. No jurist and no person checked these sessions' work. AI coding and writing assistants helped implement the harness and the product changes, run the analyses and draft the text, under the authors' direction; the authors are responsible for all of it.
