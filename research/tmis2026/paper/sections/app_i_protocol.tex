\section{Expert-study protocol}
\label{app:protocol}

This protocol is a release candidate, version 1.0-rc, to be frozen as version 1.0 before the first session; no session has taken place and this article reports no result of it. At the freeze the decision points and hashes are recorded; nothing else may then change, and any later change is a dated deviation reported with its reason. This appendix gives the protocol in compact form. The full text is \texttt{research/\allowbreak{}tmis2026/\allowbreak{}expert\_study/\allowbreak{}PREREGISTRATION\_DRAFT.md} in the repository \cite{casepath2026repo}; the fields of every record are in \texttt{expert\_study/\allowbreak{}EVENT\_FIELDS.md} beside it. Where the two texts differ, the full text holds.

\paragraph{Purpose}
Synthetic data cannot show what claims experts do with \casepath's checklist on real files. The study has five claims experts of the insurer's legal-protection unit work through three real files in the product, with their ordinary work in the interface as the only instrument. It asks five questions: Q1, how far \casepath agrees with what the insurer did on documents, step and next action, beside a single prompt given the same files, workflow and Knowledge; Q2, what five experts do with the same items; Q3, how they work with \casepath; Q4, whether \casepath improves from corrections on a case whose corrections it has not seen; and Q5, why experts change items.

\paragraph{Participants}
Five claims experts of the insurer's legal-protection unit take part; no more will be recruited. Each agrees individually, may withdraw at any time and may have their records removed. The insurer's consent is in place. No expert time is used beyond the sessions.

\paragraph{Cases}
Eleven closed files of an internal hackathon, all tenancy-defect (mould) cases with the insurer's explicit process reference. Study cases: D2, D4, D7. Reserve: D5, held back in the same way; it replaces a study case only before the first session and only if the inclusion criterion fails, the cut copy cannot be made, or a leak cannot be removed. Development files: D1, D3, D6, D8, D9, D10, D11, with development labels made by a single labeller who also builds the product; their results are reported separately and enter no test. Inclusion criterion: the three study cases route to the common workflow, checked from routing alone before the freeze. A synthetic practice case of five minutes precedes the study cases. Inference is conditional on these cases, this insurer, this process and these five experts.

\paragraph{Decision point and cut copy}
Each study case is cut at the state immediately before the insurer's first outgoing request for documents or information (to the client or the other party); without such a request, before its first outgoing action of any kind. Its date and the rule's application are recorded before any run on the cut copy. The cut copy holds only the documents or page ranges dated up to that point, and every run, view and replay uses it. The study lead checks each cut copy against the full file for leaks (later pages in merged PDFs, later notes, stamps, summaries, revealing file names); the list of included documents and the SHA-256 of the cut copy are recorded before the frozen run. A second, later decision point may be added for the system only, for all three cases, and is then exploratory.

\paragraph{Development exposure}
The study cases and the reserve are among the files the product was developed on. Four measures reduce this: the seed Knowledge is rebuilt without D2, D4, D7 and D5 and from the insurer's request letters on the development files only, and its content hash is recorded; no run on a cut copy exists before the frozen run; the commits before the seal whose change was derived from these four files are listed; and Q1 on the study cases is labelled as agreement on cases seen in development, Q4's held-out case as held out from the corrections, not from development.

\paragraph{Materials fixed before the first session}
The product commit, workflow catalogue and Knowledge store are fixed, with the parity manifest. Each cut copy is run once (the frozen run) and four more times (variation runs, never shown to an expert); each record holds the SHA-256 of the result and of the item list. During the study the Knowledge store is read-only and no model call is made in a session. Each expert works on an own copy that points to the frozen run; a session starts only if the copy's item-list hash and cut hash equal the recorded ones. The comparator is one run per cut copy of the same model with a single prompt, fixed in advance, given the same text, workflow catalogue and active Knowledge, and answering in catalogue entries (step ids, document ids, action kinds); it is scored mechanically against P. Two further comparator runs per case are made only if the internal token budget allows and are then secondary.

\paragraph{Sources of item status}
There is no gold. P, the primary reference, is not a transcription by a claims expert: the product builder's automated labeller transcribes the insurer's requests and maps them to catalogue entries before any run on the cut copy, and the study lead, an author who is not a claims expert, checks the result against the file. The sources are kept apart and never merged (Table~\ref{tab:protocol-sources}). An item is \emph{settled} only where P, P-later or O speaks to it; other items are \emph{unsettled}, shown with C and A beside them, and never called right or wrong. The structure of the process is not scored, because the workflow was built from the process reference. Whether an expert handled a case before is taken from the file's metadata, not asked.

\paragraph{Formative step}
On day~7 the process owner handles D8 at its decision point with the experts' procedure. The process owner labels nothing and takes no part in checks or decisions on the study cases. Changes made because of this session on days 7--9 are listed, with what was deliberately not changed. D8 stays a development file, and the session's events enter no analysis.

\paragraph{Session}
About 75 minutes: opening (5 minutes), practice case (5), first study case (at most 25, of which the own answer at most 10), second and third study case (at most 20 each). The caps are proposed and confirmed before the freeze. Reaching a cap is an event; the state is kept, a capped own answer is frozen as it stands, and capped cases stay in the primary analysis, flagged. The own-answer view shows the original files of the cut copy only, without model output, and offers three pick-lists: the process step (or ``not in the list''), the documents to request from the workflow's catalogue, and the next action. At the reveal the own answer is frozen with its time. Case labels A, B, C are assigned to D2, D4, D7, and expert labels E1--E5 to the experts, by a lot drawn by a person outside the research team with a recorded seed. The first case rotates (Table~\ref{tab:protocol-allocation}). Experts are asked not to discuss the cases until all sessions are done; session order is recorded.

\paragraph{Records}
Three gestures: leave an item, which closing the case as \emph{erledigt} (done) or \emph{erledigt, mit Vorbehalt} (done, with a reservation) attests; change it in place with one of six reasons in one tap, \emph{ohne Angabe} (none given) by default; and add an \emph{Anmerkung}, a free note on any screen or item, with one optional line at closing. The six reasons, shown in German, are: (1) \emph{Steht anders in den Akten}, stated differently in the file; (2) \emph{Steht nicht in den Akten}, not in the file; (3) \emph{Hier nicht relevant}, not relevant here; (4) \emph{Gilt generell so}, holds in general; (5) \emph{Weiss ich von ausserhalb der Akten}, known from outside the file; (6) \emph{Formulierung}, wording. Every action is an event in an append-only log with seconds since the session's start: case opened, item on screen (interval), source opened, item confirmed, corrected (before, after, reason), dismissed, added, correction undone, point settled, note added, own answer saved and frozen, reveal, idle start and end, letter copied (change ratio), case closed (form, attestation), cap reached, technical failure. The consequences of a correction for other items are recomputed from the log without a model and stored with the id of the correction that caused them.

\paragraph{Measures}
Each expert's final fate of each item of the frozen run is derived in this order: corrected (not undone, final value differs); dismissed; removed by propagation (excluded and counted); verified-kept (source opened or item confirmed); seen-kept (on screen for at least $d=2$ seconds in all; 1 and 4 seconds as sensitivity); unseen-kept. \emph{Changed} is corrected or dismissed. Chain items (step, gating facts, deadlines, document needs and requests, next action) are the primary universe for Q2. Denominators are always the items of the frozen run.
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1em,style=nextline,itemsep=1pt]
\item[Q1, agreement with practice] Of the documents in P, the share \casepath requests; of its requests, the share in P and, separately, in P-later; next action and step the same as P or not. Per case and pooled, for the frozen run with the range over the variation runs; the same for the comparator; secondary against O. Development files reported separately. Descriptive, no test.
\item[Q2, five experts on the same items] Fleiss' kappa and raw agreement by item kind; the share changed by at least three of five, a lower bound on \casepath's errors. Reliance on document requests, with P as status: of P's documents that \casepath lacked, the share the expert added; of its requests in neither P nor P-later, the share removed; of its requests in P, the share removed. First case: relative AI reliance and relative self-reliance over own-answer units \cite{schemmer2023appropriate}, with P in place of ground truth, as counts with denominators. Checking behaviour by fate, item kind and settled status. No reliance claim is made about facts, deadlines or document status.
\item[Q3, work] Active time per case without idle time; interventions by kind; letter change ratio; closing form against agreement with P. No effort-saving claim.
\item[Q4, learning] Leave one case out (three folds). A Knowledge proposal is made for every added document, step or question given with reason~(4) by at least two experts on the same case, or added on both training cases (matched by catalogue id). A proposal is activated only if replay on the training cases and the seven development files lowers no agreement with P and no development reference score; the gate never sees the held-out case, and Knowledge is never applied to its origin case. Primary: the change in agreement with P on the held-out case and the share of additions made there by at least three of five experts that replayed Knowledge makes unnecessary. A fall in agreement, or no proposal passing, counts against the claim.
\item[Q5, reasons] Distribution of the six reasons and of ``none given'' by item kind; reason agreement where two or more experts change the same item. Notes are counted by place; their content is grouped inside the insurer and exported without content. Run-to-run variation: for each item, the share of the five runs with the same value.
\end{description}

\paragraph{Statistics}
Every measure is given per case, per expert (unlabelled) and pooled. Intervals: bootstrap over items within case with cases fixed, 10,000 resamples with a recorded seed, percentile intervals; all five expert values and the leave-one-expert-out range; the leave-one-case-out range. One test is stated in advance. For each expert, take the share removed among \casepath's requests in neither P nor P-later, minus the share removed among its requests in P, and apply a one-sided sign test over the five experts; five of five gives $p=1/32$, the smallest attainable value, so the test can detect only a consistent direction across all or nearly all experts. A request outside practice is not thereby wrong. For Q4 the direction in each fold is reported. Every other analysis is labelled exploratory.

\paragraph{Exclusions and handling}
An incomplete session keeps its completed cases. A technical failure, a capped case, a leak found after a session and a prior-handling flag each keep the affected data, flagged, with a sensitivity analysis without it. A withdrawn expert's records are all removed and the number is reported. A session does not start on an item-list or cut-hash mismatch. Use of the reserve is reported with the replaced case and the reason.

\paragraph{Privacy}
Two exports exist. The pseudonymous export holds identifiers, statuses, counts, relative times and randomised expert labels, and no case content; a guard rejects free text. The content export (item texts, notes, the verbatim passages behind P and P-later) never leaves the insurer. No result is reported per named person, and per-expert values are shown unlabelled.

\paragraph{Limits of the design}
Five experts and three cases of one process family at one insurer bound every inference. The measured interface is not the tested one: at commit 6a72159c the interface has per-item confirmation and an edit form that requires a free-text reason, but no control for closing a case, none for a note and no coded reasons; these are being built before the freeze, so the study will measure a later version than the one tested in Section~\ref{sec:test}. The study cases are among the files the product was developed on, so agreement on them is agreement on cases seen in development. An expert may have handled a study file before, and may even have made the requests that P records; this is taken from the file's metadata and flagged, not prevented. P comes from an automated labeller built by the product's developer and is checked by an author who is not a claims expert. The process \casepath executes was built from the insurer's process reference, so its structure is not scored, and Q1 is no evidence that the process is right. Consensus forms under exposure to \casepath's result, so items changed by three of five experts bound its errors only from below. The single test cannot give $p$ below $1/32$. There will be no perception data: what experts think will be known only from what they do and note.

\paragraph{Versioning and freeze}
The freeze commit contains: the commit of the pre-registration's repository; the product commit and its parity source-tree hash; per case the decision-point date and the rule's application, the cut verification list, the cut copy's SHA-256 and the routing check; the P and P-later records with their SHA-256 and the study lead's check; the content hash of the rebuilt seed Knowledge; the development-exposure record; the formative-step change list; the SHA-256 of each frozen run's result and item list and of the variation runs; the SHA-256 of label set A; the allocation table with the lot seed; the SHA-256 of the comparator prompt; and the bootstrap seed. The frozen version is deposited with a timestamp in an internal registry and, if the venue requires, in a public registry with its content-free parts.
