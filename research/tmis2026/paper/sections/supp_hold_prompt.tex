\section{A prompt that may hold: every contrast}
\label{supp:hold-prompt}

These contrasts belong to the article's Section~\ref{M-sec:test-hold-prompt} (added after the test, on Study~3's data, not registered). They come from \texttt{study4/\allowbreak{}baseline\_\allowbreak{}check/\allowbreak{}REPORT.json}, written by \texttt{analyse.py} in that folder with Study~3's registered scorers. Difference: first design minus second, mean over cases (claims or pairs; a design's value on a case is the mean over its runs). $p$ over cases: sign-flip test over the paired case differences (exact up to 22 non-zero differences, otherwise 200{,}000 seeded sign patterns). $p$ over clusters: exact sign-flip test over the registered clusters (24 families; nine changed conditions). Lower and higher: cases in which the first design had fewer or more errors.
