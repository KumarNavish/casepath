\section{Relation to Existing Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Executable rules and evidence requirements.}
CMMN and DMN formalize case and decision state
\citep{omg2016cmmn,omg2024dmn}; LegalRuleML represents normative rules and
source associations, while Catala makes legislative computations executable
\citep{oasis2021legalruleml,merigoux2021catala}. Insurance software also
links decision tables to document lists and verification tasks
\citep{servicenow2026documentlists}. These are direct antecedents of
rule-conditioned document requirements. CasePath studies their explicit
connection to language-model assessments and evidence-planning errors.

\paragraph{Source-linked obligation control.}
\citet{kim2026evidenceobligation} separates semantic warrant judgments from
an evidence ledger with conditional obligations, duplicate suppression and
finalization; the preprint reports deployment-trace analysis rather than a
matched benchmark comparison. \citet{yu2026compile} compiles SOP constraints
into verification routines with evidence-bearing returns and exposes an
active execution frame through a stack runtime. These are close precedents
for the control principle, not merely ingredients that lacked an executable
composition. CasePath's contribution must rest on its document-planning
object and measured behavior: applicability, request permission, route
sufficiency and the relation between a source-linked plan and its errors.
The complete-corpus runtime's compiled equivalent explicitly acknowledges
that equivalent semantics need not remain stored as a graph.

\paragraph{Workflow and evidence evaluation.}
FlowBench evaluates workflow-guided planning across knowledge formats
\citep{xiao2024flowbench}; WorFBench evaluates generated workflows
\citep{qiao2024worfbench}; PAGED evaluates document-to-procedural-graph
extraction \citep{du2024paged}. These tasks motivate structural evaluation,
but supply no current-method transfer result here. The native CasePath
measurements additionally distinguish complete evidence coverage from
unnecessary demands and structural chains from exact source support.
ReAct interleaves reasoning and environment actions \citep{yao2022react},
whereas Study~B is tool-free planning. Its shared review opportunity is
also not a reproduction of Chain-of-Verification's independent verification
questions \citep{dhuliawala2024chain}. The named experimental comparators
are custom conditions with their own source and prompt identities.

\paragraph{Grounding and information seeking.}
GraphCompliance aligns policy and context graphs for regulatory assessment
\citep{chung2025graphcompliance}. CLER evaluates evidence lists and the legal
facts they support from claims and factual premises \citep{bi2026cler}.
InfoGatherer combines retrieval and targeted questions using uncertainty
in an evidential network \citep{taranukhin2026infogatherer}. Verified-quotation
methods make textual support inspectable \citep{menick2022verifiedquotes}.
CasePath's paired intervention isolates a further behavioral question:
whether a changed branch fact changes the appropriate document demands.

\section{State Updates, Replay and the Agentic Workbench}
\label{sec:product}

The six-role workbench organizes Facts, Orchestration, Source Integrity,
Process, Evidence and Audit/Readiness around one authoritative case record.
Roles make source inspection, handoffs, mappings and readiness decisions
visible; they are not six independent versions of the case or necessarily
six model calls. A new admitted observation can trigger a recomputation
through Equation~\eqref{eq:loop}. This stateful inspection surface complements
the experiments, but its operational acceptance is not a model-performance
endpoint.

The archived V5 parity report records agreement on document sets, semantic
guard states and next actions for 72 units, and on all 36 pair deltas. Its
verifier supplies the product runtime with the previously recorded guard
answers through a replay callback. The supported statement is therefore
\emph{downstream product/research agreement conditional on recorded
assessments}. It is not fresh batched-versus-singleton inference agreement,
a 150-case six-role result, or proof of live deployment identity.

The native comparison uses a different shared planning service and compact
assessment/artifact interface. Its code-level correspondence and the
authored teaching guide do not inherit V5's 72/72 certificate. Likewise,
neither experiment establishes expert agreement, customer benefit, successful
autonomous acquisition or validated transfer to another domain. The common
architectural idea is source-linked state-dependent planning; its concrete
implementations, evidence and limits remain separately inspectable.
