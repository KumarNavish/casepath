\paragraph{Branch concepts.}
The \nConcepts{} concepts cover a scheduled valuable at or above the
source-defined threshold; a stolen bicycle or e-bike; SIM-card theft with
a misuse claim; an identifiable third-party causer; selection of formal
expert proceedings; contemplated repairs above the consent threshold;
a written demand with a forfeiture warning and period; recovery of, or
news about, stolen items; and insurer declination. Each appears in four
neutral contexts. Only the branch statement changes within a pair.

\paragraph{Signed-change scoring.}
\label{app:paired-metrics}
For pair $i$, the scorer compares prediction $P_i=\Delta(D_i)$ with every
acceptable signed reference realisation $G\in\mathcal G_i$. It selects the
maximum of the lexicographic tuple
$\bigl(F_1(P_i,G),\,|P_i\cap G|,\,\mathrm{Prec}(P_i,G),\,\mathrm{Rec}(P_i,G),\,-|G|,\,\mathrm{sort}(G)\bigr)$.
The final component supplies the frozen deterministic tie-break. Precision
and recall are zero when their denominators are empty; $F_1$ is one when
both the prediction and reference are empty. Micro scores pool counts for
the selected realisations across \nPairsHid{} pairs. Family scores average
pair $F_1$ within each of the nine concepts.

For each of \bootDraws{} bootstrap draws, we sample nine whole families
with replacement using seed \bootSeed. Each selected family retains all
three of its held-out contexts. The exact family-swap test enumerates
\swapAssignments{} sign assignments of the nine family mean differences.
Its three one-sided $p$-values, \pRawDir, \pRawGc{} and \pRawEf, become
\pHolmDir, \pHolmGc{} and \pHolmEf{} after Holm adjustment. The
wrong-pairing diagnostic uses \wpShifts{} cyclic shifts and \wpDraws{}
sampled derangements, with add-one score
$(1+N_{\ge})/(1+\wpEvaluated)$. Its reported maximum covers the evaluated
reassignments only.

\paragraph{Freeze and split handling.}
The method freeze \path{casepath.theft-method-freeze/5.0.0} binds the source
bytes, benchmark v3 and both amendments, proposition-complete refinement,
document mapping, interpreter V4 and planner V3. It also binds the model,
provider and temperature settings, analysis code, development split,
matched comparators' raw predictions and focused method tests.
Development used \nPairsDev{} pairs (\nUnitsDev{} units), one context per
concept. Its predeclared admission checks authorised one read of the
remaining \nPairsHid{} pairs (\nUnitsHid{} units) under a separate
hash-bound execution freeze. The method remained locked after that read;
no scientific file was adapted to the held-out outcomes.

\paragraph{Product parity.}
The verifier loads the installed, hash-bound method pack and the product
service, then replays all \parityUnits{} units with the recorded guard
decisions. It compares verdicts, document sets and next actions, checks
all \parityPairs{} pair deltas through the product's diff surface, and
verifies that every rendered quotation occurs exactly in its source passage.
These comparisons establish implementation identity; an HTTP success code
or a plausible demonstration alone would not do so.

\paragraph{Error-origin partition.}
The retrospective partition forms the union of reference documents from
unit annotations and all acceptable signed changes. Each arm retains its
original scorer-selected realisation, so its signed-change counts stay
unchanged. The bicycle-voucher amendment updated acceptable deltas but left
two auxiliary vocabulary fields unchanged. Including both definitions gives
the same error counts. Changing one sentence can affect several inferred
guards, so the observed absence of withdrawals does not establish
monotonicity. The release contains the partition script, all counts and
the field-consistency note.
