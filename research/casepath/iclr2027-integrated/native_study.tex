\section{Study B: The Complete Released Corpus}
\label{sec:native}

\paragraph{Corpus and arms.}
Study~B evaluates \nbClaims{} synthetic tenancy claims in \nbFamilies{}
families across three domains (defect, mould and heating; lease termination;
rent increase). The development split holds \nbDevClaims{} claims in
\nbDevFamilies{} families; the protected split holds \nbHidClaims{} claims in
\nbHidFamilies{} disjoint families. Five learned arms share one
deterministically compiled public representation and the same observable
case packet, with \nbCallsPerCell{} calls per cell (draft and review),
\nbReasoning{} reasoning, \nbTokens{} completion tokens per call, no tools
and no resampling, on the same model alias as Study~A. \casepath predicts an
assessment that code compiles through
Equations~\ref{eq:native-activation}--\ref{eq:native-route}; the reviewed
Direct, Document-first, Process-context and Rule-first arms generate the
artefact themselves. Two dependent controls, a compiled equivalent and a
local-scope ablation, replay \casepath's assessment through alternative
executions, giving \nbLearnedCells{} learned and \nbDependentCells{}
dependent cells, \nbCells{} in all. Because the arms differ in what the model
emits, Study~B is a pipeline comparison under matched information and budget,
not an isolated test of graph storage or prompting order.

\paragraph{Endpoints and contract.}
The native evaluator scores graph structure, branch and path probes, evidence
obligations, chains, document states and exact source locators; a separate
endpoint counts the immediate requests a system actually emitted
(Appendix~\ref{app:native-metrics}). Three primary endpoints are reported
against each learned comparator: emitted-checklist $F_1$, critical-evidence
recall and the fraction of unnecessary requests. The frozen contract fixes
descriptive estimation on the protected split with domains, families and
cases weighted equally; conservative contrasts assign $-1$ whenever either
endpoint is unobserved and must jointly exceed the boundaries $\bndF$,
$\bndCer$ and $\bndUdr$ before a comparator counts as beaten; single-family
deletion ranges replace confidence intervals, and no $p$-values are computed.
Failed, truncated or blocked cells stay in the denominator with adverse
penalties. All \nbClaims{} inputs were inspected during product work, so
``protected'' denotes custody and family disjointness, not untouched-input
generalisation.

\paragraph{Execution record.}
Prediction freezes precede all scoring, and no score exists until both splits
are frozen. All \nbRecorded{} scheduled cells are recorded, \nbDevCells{} on
the development split and \nbHidRecorded{} on the protected split. The
development record alone already exposes a property of the interface
(Table~\ref{tab:native-execution}).
Under the same budget, the generative arms fail schema validation in
\bDirDevFail, \bDocDevFail, \bPcDevFail{} and \bRfDevFail{} of \nbDevClaims{}
cells, against \bCpDevFail{} for \casepath. Their dominant failure is an
artefact relation whose endpoint does not exist (\bDirDevEndpoint,
\bDocDevEndpoint, \bPcDevEndpoint{} and \bRfDevEndpoint{} cells), a failure
\casepath cannot produce because it emits an assessment that code turns into
a graph. Truncation at the completion limit follows (\bDirDevTrunc,
\bDocDevTrunc, \bPcDevTrunc{} and \bRfDevTrunc{} against \bCpDevTrunc).
\casepath's own failures are contradictory presence states
(\bCpDevPresence) and invalid codebook indices (\bCpDevCodebook). These are
execution outcomes, not quality scores, and they penalise every arm alike in
the final report.

\paragraph{What the design supports.}
Figure~\ref{fig:native-case} shows the mechanism on one recorded case. When
both freezes are complete, the frozen code produces a single descriptive
finite-corpus report, and the release contains the contract, producer,
evaluator and every recorded cell so that the report can be regenerated
rather than trusted. A positive report would show that explicit obligation
execution yields better-justified complete plans than generative pipelines
given the same compiled information on this corpus, and nothing about
population superiority, mechanism isolation or unseen-input generalisation;
it is not pooled with Study~A.
