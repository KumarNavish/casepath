\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Executable rules and case state.}
\casepath builds on the established separation of rules from the state to
which they apply. LegalRuleML records norms and their source associations
\citep{oasis2021legalruleml}, and Catala makes legislative computation
executable \citep{merigoux2021catala}. DMN formalises decisions; CMMN
represents case work activated by guards \citep{omg2024dmn,omg2016cmmn}.
Guard--Stage--Milestone models and DCR reference models of law connect
activation driven by data to compliance
\citep{vaculin2011gsm,lopez2020dcrlaw}. Proactive enforcement derives actions
from active obligations \citep{basin2024proactive}, while insurance software
conditions document lists on service definitions
\citep{servicenow2026documentlists}. We combine this approach with
language-model assessments and measure the resulting evidence-planning
errors.

\paragraph{Procedural graphs and workflow agents.}
PAGED extracts procedural graphs from documents \citep{du2024paged}.
BREX evaluates business-rule flows with executable grounding
\citep{yang2026brex}, and Guideline2Graph compiles clinical guidelines into
decision graphs that retain provenance \citep{kilic2026guideline2graph}.
\citet{graus2026decisionmodels} evaluate structured representations for
legal decision models; CANONIC treats governance as compilation
\citep{hadley2026canonic}. FlowBench and WorFBench evaluate workflow-guided
planning and workflow generation \citep{xiao2024flowbench,qiao2024worfbench}.
Procedural Graphs place an agent at an active node
\citep{lu2026proceduralgraphs}, while ReAct interleaves reasoning and action
\citep{yao2022react}. Our experiments ask whether changing a case fact changes
exactly the evidence justified by the affected branch.

\paragraph{Evidence obligations and acquisition.}
\citet{kim2026evidenceobligation} provide the closest evidence-ledger
precedent: branch conditions activate knowledge obligations, a model judges
semantic warrant and code manages closure. \citet{yu2026compile} compile
SOP constraints into verification routines with evidence-bearing returns
and an active execution frame. We study these principles for document
planning, where applicability and acquisition permission can differ, a
present document may be inadequate, and several documents may need joint
assessment. The representation retains alternative evidence routes, and
the benchmark measures their effect on requests. Neither prior system is
reproduced as an experimental arm, so our comparisons do not establish
superiority over them.

\paragraph{Grounding and information seeking.}
GraphCompliance aligns policy and context graphs for regulatory assessment
\citep{chung2025graphcompliance}. CLER scores litigation evidence lists and
the facts they prove \citep{bi2026cler}. InfoGatherer uses evidential
uncertainty to select retrieval and questions
\citep{taranukhin2026infogatherer}. Verified quotations and
chain-of-verification expose the support for a response
\citep{menick2022verifiedquotes,dhuliawala2024chain}. API-Bank and BFCL
evaluate function selection in tool use \citep{li2023apibank,patil2025bfcl}.
Our measured object is the justification for requesting evidence in a
particular case.

\paragraph{Evaluation validity.}
Work on partial-input baselines, annotation artefacts and shortcut learning
motivates our input-free and wrong-pairing controls
\citep{kaushik2018reading,poliak2018hypothesis,gururangan2018artifacts,mccoy2019hans,sugawara2018easier,geirhos2020shortcut,bowman2021benchmarking,yuan2024shortcut}.
We authored Study~A's references from the sources. No model adjudicated them,
which avoids the preference leakage that can arise when the judged and
judging models coincide \citep{li2026preference}. Evaluation-validity research
\citep{thomas2026validity} and critiques of legal benchmarks focused on
outcome prediction instead of practitioners' work
\citep{medvedeva2023right,lou2026lawyering} motivate our use of signed
document changes and emitted requests as outcomes.
