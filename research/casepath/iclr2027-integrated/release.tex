\section{Release: One Planner for Research and Product}
\label{sec:release}

A research method and a claims interface can silently diverge. The Study~A
release packages the frozen representation, propositions, snapshot, method
manifest, benchmark identity and model configuration into a hash-bound
method pack; the client cannot inject rules, mappings or targets, and hash
drift makes the endpoint fail closed. The product calls the same guard
interpreter and planner as the experiment and exposes guard states, chains,
questions, gaps, requests, exact source spans and the next action. Replaying
all \parityUnits{} benchmark units with the recorded guard decisions
reproduces the research document sets, guard states and next actions on
every unit and all \parityPairs{} signed pair changes, with every rendered
quotation an exact substring of its passage: implementation identity
conditional on recorded assessments, not fresh-inference agreement or
deployment evidence. The workbench organises six roles (Facts, Orchestration,
Source Integrity, Process, Evidence, and Audit and Readiness) around one case
record, and a newly admitted observation triggers recomputation through
Equation~\ref{eq:loop}. The release also contains benchmark versions,
predictions, receipts, analysis code, the parity verifier, and Study~B's
corpus, producer, evaluator, contract and every recorded cell. Both the
registered primary failure and the separate request-only diagnostic reproduce
offline from the exact released reference containers. Reference targets and
authored teaching states are displayed separately from model predictions;
neither is additional benchmark evidence.
