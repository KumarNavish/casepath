\section*{Reproducibility Statement}
The release contains, for Study~A, the frozen snapshot, propositions, rules,
document map, interpreter, planner, benchmark versions, all predictions,
provider receipts, analysis code, the preregistered gate and the product
replay verifier, and for Study~B its corpus, compiled representation,
producer, evaluator, analysis contract and every recorded cell, including
failures. Every number in this paper is generated from those files by a
script that records the file hash and JSON pointer behind each value. The
release commands recompute Study~A and both Study~B reports from the recorded
outputs and compare them with the preserved measurements without network or
provider access (Appendices~\ref{app:studya} and~\ref{app:request-diagnostic}). Reproducing scores from recorded outputs
is distinct from regenerating model outputs, which requires provider access
and is not deterministic.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
\casepath is a research architecture for evidence planning, not legal advice
or an automated coverage decision. The studies use public-source snapshots
and synthetic cases with no personal data. Real deployment requires policy
and jurisdiction scoping, professional review, privacy controls and a
mechanism for correction and appeal.

\section*{AI Use Statement}
Generative AI tools were used in hypothesis formulation, methodological
critique, literature discovery, implementation and testing, synthetic-case
construction, diagnostic interpretation, and drafting and editing of text and
figures. The reproducibility materials distinguish actual executions,
engineering fixtures, archived reports and author judgements; failed results
and version differences are retained. The authors are responsible for the
final scientific claims, citations, measurements and artefacts.
