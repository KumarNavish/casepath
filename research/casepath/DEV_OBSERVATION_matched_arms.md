# First matched comparison — a development observation, n = 1

**Status: development, not evidence.** One case, written by me, on the development scope, with the same
graph standing in for both the induced and reference arms. It is recorded because it is the first time the
arms have been run against each other under matched conditions, and because the separation is in the
direction the thesis predicts. It is not a result and will not be reported as one.

## The case

A rent increase from CHF 1,450 to CHF 1,610 notified by ordinary letter, with no official form, giving only
"adjustment to market rent" as the reason. Under OR 269d and the tenancy ordinance, an increase not
notified on the official cantonal form with reasons is **void**, and the tenant has 30 days from
notification to challenge.

So the two legally decisive documents are the notice itself — because whether it is the official form
determines nullity — and evidence of when it was received, because that starts the 30-day window.

## What each arm asked for

| arm | requests | documents |
|---|---|---|
| B1 direct | 1 | landlord correspondence |
| B2 retrieval + direct | 1 | envelope copy, landlord correspondence |
| B3 graph, independent list | 1 | landlord correspondence |
| B3t prose summary, independent list | 1 | envelope copy, landlord correspondence, **rent-increase official form** |
| B6 prior composition | 1 | landlord correspondence |
| **B5 process-derived** | **3** | **rent-increase official form, registered-mail receipt**, tenant correspondence |

Four of the five comparison arms asked only for "landlord correspondence" — a generic request that would
not settle either decisive question. The process-derived arm asked for the form and the receipt, which are
the two documents the law makes material, and produced 27 justification chains of which 15 are clean.

**B3 is the informative one.** Giving the model the graph and letting it write the checklist independently
produced the same answer as no graph at all. The prose-summary arm did better than the graph arm, which
suggests that on this case extra reasoning helped more than structure did — unless the structure is what
*derives* the list rather than being shown alongside it. That is precisely the coupling the paper's central
ablation is meant to isolate, and one case cannot settle it.

## What this does not show

n = 1, on a case I wrote, on the development scope, with the reference graph identical to the induced one,
scored by me reading the output rather than against an independent contract. Every one of those is fixed in
the experiment being assembled: source-based reference contracts built without reference to the system,
cases drawn from the corpus rather than authored, a held-out scope admitted under a frozen rule, and the
arms scored by the evaluator rather than by inspection.
