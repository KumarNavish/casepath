# Closest work (primary sources checked 2026-09-14; operations, not names)

| work | what it supplies | relation to CTES |
|---|---|---|
| EnvTrustBench — "When Agents Overtrust Environmental Evidence" (arXiv:2605.08828v2, May 2026) | Defines evidence-grounding defects (an agent treats an environment-facing claim as sufficient evidence for action without resolving it against current evidence); 55 generated cases, 11 scenarios, 6 backbones, 5 scaffolds; no mitigation proposed. | Names the phenomenon CTES targets. CTES supplies the missing mechanism: level is fixed by channel metadata, so a claim can never become the artifact. |
| "Trust, but Don't Verify: Epistemic Blind Spots in LLM Source Evaluation" (arXiv:2606.05403, Jul 2026) | Models judge validity in isolation but suppress it during multi-source synthesis; prompting and oracle checklists produce blanket skepticism, not selective discernment; six models, four families. | Explains why prompt-level rules (E76 ledger prompt) fail and why the epistemic level must be removed from the model's synthesis step. CTES asks the model only for local content atoms. |
| ProvenanceGuard (arXiv:2606.18037, Jun 2026) | Decompose answer into atomic claims → route to source-specific evidence → NLI support → attribution comparison → allow/block; targets cross-source conflation. | Nearest algorithmic neighbour (decompose-then-verify). Content entailment cannot separate "the notice says D" reported by the customer from the notice itself; CTES's channel cap is metadata-typed and action-oriented (readiness, requests), not a factuality verdict. Implemented fairly as B5/ablation (same atoms, no cap). |
| An Evidence Model for Agentic Processes (arXiv:2609.08481, Sep 2026) | Conceptual taxonomy of 14 evidence-claim types for agentic systems; no inference procedure or experiments. | Confirms the claim-type distinctions are recognised; CTES operationalises a 3-level subset with a computable update and decision rule. |
| Dynamic logics of evidence-based belief (van Benthem & Pacuit 2011); Evidence logic | Neighbourhood semantics for beliefs from possibly conflicting sources; evidence management actions. | Semantic backdrop; no channel typing of sources, no acquisition policy. |
| Best evidence rule / hearsay (FRE 1002, 801) | Content of a writing is proved by the writing; out-of-court statements are not proof of their content. | The legal ancestor of the channel cap; CTES is its agent-side computational form with an acquisition consequence. |
| CGDP (arXiv:2605.07042), InfoGatherer (arXiv:2603.05909), EC²/HEC, adaptive cover | Belief-state context gathering, evidential networks, decision-region acquisition. | Given a correctly specified observation model they subsume acquisition; they do not supply the observation model. CTES supplies it generically from channel metadata (fixed-core transfer tests exactly this). |
| E76 decision ledger + compiler (CasePath, Sep 2026) | Model-declared decisions/states compiled into requests and readiness. | Strongest existing CasePath structure (B4); consumed model-declared status and failed carrier 043 under Opus. |
