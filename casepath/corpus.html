What this view means

Each message and attachment name comes from the frozen observable packet. The list does not contain the correct process path, required documents or a claim outcome. A filename is not proof of what a document establishes.

Open a claim in the local workbench to inspect original file bytes, record observations and follow the handling path. The 28 scenario families and the development versus protected-family split are described in the data card; those study labels are deliberately absent from the intake view.
