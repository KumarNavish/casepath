Three distinctions that change the plan.

      
01
Unresolved is a state.
An unresolved branch creates a question. It does not silently become false, and it does not authorize an immediate document request.

      
02
Held does not mean sufficient.
A file must establish the required fact. When its adequacy is unknown, the controller calls for review. Multi-document routes also require a joint assessment.

      
03
Alternatives are alternatives.
An adequate route satisfies the capability. Otherwise, the controller selects a route per capability, prioritizing fewer requests, then fewer reviews. It does not optimize a global document set.
